\section{Accountability}
\label{sec:explainer:accountability}

Accountability is about the channel and the charge, never the lean. If your action moves someone's fate, you answer for the movement --- even if you feel nothing, even if your feelings cancel, even if your private weighting hides the tie. The careless landlord who ``feels nothing'' for a tenant still holds the tenant's winter in his hands; the not-feeling changes nothing about the holding. And the structure is cleanly two-stage: the \emph{gate} is capacity --- could anything move through this edge at all? --- and it shuts only for the genuine stranger, where the channel itself is zero; the \emph{charge} is what this act actually moved. An act that happens to miss today, over a live channel, leaves you answerable still: the gate never consulted the act.

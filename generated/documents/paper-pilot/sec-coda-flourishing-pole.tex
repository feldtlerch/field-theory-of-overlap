\begin{econ}[label={e:capability},title={The flourishing pole},ref={op:vmoral},cites={a:sencapability},departs={annex},stopsat={capability is wellbeing as freedom-to-achieve, a state; virtue-as-enlargement is the rate that moves it, the information welfarism discards}]The flourishing pole is Sen's capability approach \parencite{a:sencapability}: wellbeing as freedom to achieve functionings, not utility and not the record. Virtue-as-enlargement is enlarged freedom-to-achieve, relational and unscalarizable --- exactly the information welfarism discards. The genealogy reads as one shape: economics builds outward from the funded interior and stops, each time, at a different face of one wall --- the input it cannot move, the aggregate that does not exist, the score that is a value-theory, the judge that is built not detached, the testimony it cannot de-colour, the cycle that rotates only when coupling is directed, the standing less aggregable than price, the edge that is a rate, the restraint priced only at the menu, the payroll it cannot total, the flourishing that is the history-metric.\end{econ}

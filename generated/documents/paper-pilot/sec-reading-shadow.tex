\PaperNativeParagraph{sec:reading:shadow}{One may assume naively that the fact is the light and the principle its elaboration; the dependence runs the other way --- the fact is the shadow the principle casts on the poorest instrument, and the still center's population is scoped by exactly that dependence: darkness is the set of held, unposted converters, with the unspendable ground beneath (\ftag{src:nd}).}

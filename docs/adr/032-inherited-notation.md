# ADR 032: Inherited notation before graph binding · `rec:adr:032-inherited-notation`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:notation:inherited-before-binding`

The comovfield, readout, willvirtue and gensym source closure is admitted ahead of its first converted scope; a further inherited definition enters the closed notation catalogue through the existing checked expression language when a converted scope first needs it, with signature, decoded body, complete source dependency closure and comparison evidence admitted together. No opaque TeX expression or independently authored screen formula is admitted. An inherited definition has no theory object or backing and remains pending with a missing-binding reason, which every definition using it retains. `generated/notation-status.json` lists every inherited definition and its pending reason under M049. A symbol whose only pending reasons are inherited missing-binding reasons renders without a pending mark; a symbol with any other pending reason keeps its mark. Until its source file is replaced under ADR 030 (`rec:adr:030-native-renderings-complete-graph-native-model`), paper typesets the inherited definition through its existing source declaration invoked by name; checked source comparison holds that declaration's body equal to the admitted expression, while the screen form derives from that expression and passes ADR 022's strict contract (`rec:adr:022-markdown-mathematics`). The named quantity, metric and registered-variance declarations retain their signatures and checks, and checking effects remain outside notation bodies. Multi-letter text is carried by the identity of a reviewed nullary primitive, never a widened literal or new AST leaf. Unknown syntax, dependency, operation, effect or consuming context refuses the scope. Graph binding converts an inherited entry to a canonical or alias entry; the two never coexist. This preserves ADR 004's closed expression language (`rec:adr:004-notation-ast`) through the complete-graph boundary and relationship disposition governed by ADR 036 (`rec:adr:036-relationship-dispositions`).

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:notation:inherited-reasons`

Rendering can precede theory admission without silently granting a theory object. A named paper invocation preserves quantity accounting and registered variance that visual expansion alone could bypass. Source comparison binds the existing paper declaration to the checked expression that generates the screen form; the dependency closure makes a revision detectable. Pending evidence tells reviewers what remains unbound without changing familiar formulas in the reader's copy.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:notation:inherited-declined`

Binding every inherited symbol to a theory object before rendering couples conversion to theory admission. Raw TeX, separately authored screen formulas and copied checking annotations create additional authorities. Decorating every inherited formula as pending changes the visual text without strengthening the missing binding evidence.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:notation:inherited-consequences`

The following ADR 004 sentences remain verbatim; each insertion follows the quoted sentence and applies only to inherited definitions.

|Existing sentence|Insertion|
|---|---|
|“Notation is generated from a closed abstract syntax: primitives with declared arity from a registry, literals that reject structural characters, calls matched to declared signatures.”|“Until inherited source declarations are replaced, their paper bodies remain source-authored under checked comparison with the admitted expression; their screen bodies derive from the closed syntax.”|
|“Symbol tiers (authorised against pending) are derived from the graph, never curated, with an alias taking the lesser tier of its own and its target's; an object has one canonical symbol; notation constructors are not quantity commands, and dimensions render with plain brackets.”|“An inherited definition without a theory object derives pending status from the missing binding; canonical and alias requirements for object-bound definitions and the constructor rules remain unchanged.”|
|“A quantity without a backing object stays visibly pending, naming what it awaits.”|“A symbol pending only for missing bindings of inherited definitions instead names those bindings in generated status evidence and has no visible pending mark; other pending reasons retain their visible marks.”|
|“Admitting a primitive is a reviewed registry change checked against what already composes; the paper's full symbol vocabulary enters only as fast as the referent expansion carries it.”|“The recorded four-definition source closure entered ahead of its first converted scope; further inherited definitions enter when a converted scope first requires them, before their referents have theory objects, provided their decoded bodies and complete dependency closures pass the same checked expression language; their graph bindings remain outstanding.”|

The sentence “notation constructors are not quantity commands” remains part of ADR 004's first quoted sentence; inherited paper invocations preserve declared quantity interfaces rather than turning constructors into quantities. ADR 004's argument about checks written in a typesetting language does not withdraw existing inherited checking declarations. ADR 022's strict expansion and context refusals remain unchanged. A source definition or dependency revision invalidates comparison evidence and requires readmission before use.

**Amendment (Rule text)** · `amend:notation:inherited-rule-amendments`

Each existing statement below is copied verbatim from the rule register at this record's admission. Its insertion follows that sentence except for M056, whose replacement takes the existing sentence's place. Scope changes are stated after the table; all existing rule states remain unchanged.

|Rule|Existing statement|Exact insertion|
|---|---|---|

|M051|Both notation forms derive from the same checked AST with explicit token boundaries and within the output limit; full Unicode expansion contains no project commands and every returned expression passes the pinned KaTeX grammar.| For inherited definitions instead, the paper form invokes the existing checked source declaration by name, whose body is source-compared with the admitted AST, and the strict screen form derives from that AST; pending marks and visible pending explanations are omitted only when every pending reason is an inherited missing binding.|

The native assembly sweep includes every derived notation case except those owned by inherited definitions, including all primitive cases. Inherited definitions have no generated declaration there because paper uses their existing source declarations, so inherited definitions have no native-sweep rows. M078 holds their paper forms through checked source comparison, and the strict KaTeX sweep holds their screen forms. Binding an inherited definition to its graph object replaces its catalogue entry with a canonical or alias entry; the body-equality check alone does not establish that future conversion.

M047's existing definition-body scope covers inherited signatures and decoded bodies. M048's scope already includes absent objects and unused definitions. M049's scope adds inherited missing bindings, their transitive uses and `generated/notation-status.json`. M050's scope changes from every emitted notation declaration and its checked KaTeX context to every emitted notation declaration and temporary inherited declaration used in the checked KaTeX context, including expression validation. M051's scope adds the named inherited paper invocation and checked source comparison exception while keeping fully expanded screen expressions. M052's scope adds temporary inherited declarations and screen forms within the KaTeX sweep. M056's scope changes from prepared LaTeX documents, their actual preamble and support files, and every native sweep case to those documents and every derived notation sweep case except those owned by inherited definitions. M057's scope changes from generated Markdown and LaTeX documents, notation.tex and addressing.tex to those documents and support files plus notation-status.json. M078 states: “Each inherited source declaration has the admitted signature, decoded visual body and complete dependency closure, with named quantity, metric and registered-variance interfaces unchanged. Source syntax and its checked expression form a reviewed pairing, and each inherited definition has exactly one compared source declaration site among the recognised declaration and redefinition forms in the three named source files. Registered-variance declaration sites for each inherited name across those files equal its recorded count.” Its scope is: “Every admitted inherited declaration in `documents/paper/data/ftoo_symbols.tex`, `documents/paper/data/ftoo_symbols_historical.tex` and `documents/paper/data/ftoo_registers.tex`, including its checked source bytes and declared checking interface; declaration sites in other files are outside this comparison.” The counted command words are `\NewDocumentCommand`, `\RenewDocumentCommand`, `\DeclareDocumentCommand`, `\ProvideDocumentCommand`, `\NewExpandableDocumentCommand`, `\RenewExpandableDocumentCommand`, `\DeclareExpandableDocumentCommand`, `\ProvideExpandableDocumentCommand`, `\DeclareRobustCommand`, `\NewCommandCopy`, `\RenewCommandCopy`, `\DeclareCommandCopy`, `\DeclareQuantity`, `\newcommand`, `\renewcommand`, `\providecommand`, `\def`, `\gdef`, `\edef`, `\xdef`, `\let` and `\cs_set:Npn`; their targets may use an optional star or brace, and an explicit `\csname` target counts only after one of those definers. Unescaped comments do not count. Registered-variance sites are counted separately for each inherited name across the same three files: one for comovfield and none for readout, willvirtue or gensym. An unsupported command, context, cycle, wrong arity or limit violation refuses admission. Source comparisons for the initial closure are recorded in [Inherited source comparison](../../tools/notation/inherited-source-comparison.md).

The three compared source files remain embedded in the notation catalogue because admission currently has no document-input path for them. Any edit to those files invalidates the dependent Rust record, whose changed-record path calls `cargo clean -p` for ftoo_codegen, ftoo_manifest and ftoo_audit before a release rebuild; one release build with the pinned toolchain, `nice -n19` and at most four jobs measured 46.59 seconds, but the target cache's cold or warm state was not recorded, and the paper-data edit and preceding clean were excluded. A document-input path would have to pass the three source texts into catalogue admission and declare them in the document receipt before this embedding is removed.

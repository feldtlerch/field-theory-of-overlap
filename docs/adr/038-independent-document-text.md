# ADR 038: Independent complete-document text comparison · `rec:adr:038-independent-document-text`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:paper:independent-document-text`

Requested conversion comparisons use captured complete PDFs and a recorded extractor, compare their whole reader-text streams and ordered bookmark titles under explicitly checked normalization rules, and retain raw pages and all exclusions. A checked source correspondence corroborates converted content without selecting what the document comparison reads. A line-end hyphen stays in each complete stream. Unequal line-end events for a hyphen, en dash, em dash or slash on equal streams refuse; reflow that moves a line end onto or off one of these marks refuses when the normalized streams stay equal, and a discretionary hyphen that alone separates the streams refuses. The same mark at the same line-end position on both sides does not establish whether a hyphen is lexical or discretionary or whether a following space was authored. Within the corroborated paragraph, only that side's decoded source may confirm a joined word and permit removing its hyphen for correspondence. A checked bare paper tag uses the extracted small-cap spelling measured on the delivered pair, and correspondence refuses a mismatch; case inside small capitals is compared as extracted, without general case folding, so a source case change there can compare equal. Invalid inputs, unavailable or ambiguous extraction and unsupported normalization refuse; unequal compared streams are reported as different, and reflow after non-letter punctuation or moved floats can yield that verdict without changing words. A line-end mark the profile does not join refuses correspondence unless its source has the extracted spacing. Equality is comparison evidence, not acceptance, a proof of mathematical equivalence, paragraph boundaries or a claim that all rendering changes are visible in extracted text.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:paper:independent-document-text-reasons`

Whole-document coverage exposes words added outside a chosen paragraph. Raw text avoids spaces manufactured by a font-size change. Verified furniture separates page numbering from reader content without erasing authored marks. A source-confirmed discretionary hyphen permits correspondence without erasing a lexical hyphen or changing the complete-stream comparison. The delivered pair establishes the tag font's small-cap text mapping; comparison uses the extracted case of all small-capital text. Captured bytes and extractor identities make the result describe what was read.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:paper:independent-document-text-declined`

Selected spans can omit the change under examination. Blanket number removal, repeated-line suppression and unconfirmed hyphen deletion can erase assertions. General case folding can erase a change in running text. Source-only comparison misses typeset words; PDF text alone misses structure and checking effects. Parent-byte identity as a universal conversion condition mistakes stable layout for preserved content.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:paper:independent-document-text-consequences`

The comparison retains complete raw evidence and reports its bounded coverage. Unrecognized extraction or normalization refuses rather than supplying acceptance. Same-input reproduction remains required. A tooling change predicting unchanged PDF bytes investigates a failed prediction before retention; parent-byte equality is not a general conversion requirement. Formula structure, target bodies, styles, link destinations and checking effects require evidence beyond this text comparison. Page and screen acceptance remain separate human judgments under the records governing them; this record changes no acceptance authority or timing.

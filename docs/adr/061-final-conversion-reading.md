# ADR 061: Native conversion and complete final reading · `rec:adr:061-final-conversion-reading`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:final-conversion-reading`

Native conversion requires current-product checks, without parent-to-candidate correspondence proof or individual page and screen acceptance. Two independent complete final readings replace mechanical conversion eligibility and residual-only final review. A content batch changes no kind, logical order, meaning or identity; such a change takes ordinary review under ADR 026. A deliberate changed or lost check requires the project's owner's explicit approval.

The renderings remain generated presentations of one source. This decision admits no new grammar, context or admission by containment. TeX and PDF bytes may differ; reader content and claims are preserved, and reflow is not checked. At completion the existing checking evidence owners compare inputs, effects and outcomes within their declared coverage; unavailable coverage is stated, not inferred from appearance.

Both final readers read the complete paper and explainer independently before exchanging findings. They are not authors of converted content and have different model families from each other; machinery authorship is permitted. The reference remains v0.6.0-dev.2 with its recorded wording renewal; the new documents are the fixed completed release candidate.

The PDF reading unit is a page with its corresponding pages or page portions on the other side, matched by content in reading order, not page number. Each reader covers every old and new page. Findings record missing, added or changed material; major page movements and appearance are judged once at completion. For each screen rendering, the unit is the rendered section or contiguous reading block corresponding to that PDF content, including complete tables, displays, lists and notes. Both readers read Markdown and HTML in full against the old and new PDF content; the old incomplete screens are not a completeness reference.

Each reader records artifact versions, coverage, located findings and a verdict. Findings are united, including disagreements. Theory-claim findings go to the project's owner; other findings receive correction and ordinary review. A reader checks corrections in their affected context. Completion requires both readings, disposition of all findings and the existing release requirements.

Keep the artifact identities, readers and model families, coverage, findings, dispositions and final acceptance, including the nineteen passage renewals; no reproducible run archive is required. Optional whole-document word reports using the existing extraction may compare old and new PDFs and each new screen with the new PDF; they assist reading and never gate retention or release.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:final-conversion-reading-reasons`

Complete reading judges the result that readers receive. Two independent readers from different model families reduce shared blind spots without treating agreement as proof. Per-transfer source coordinates and extraction order constrain movement without establishing that the completed document reads correctly. Current generation and semantic checks still protect properties a page reading cannot see. One combined generation avoids conflicting derived edits without abandoning reproducibility.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:final-conversion-reading-declined`

Per-transfer correspondence gates and final review limited to their residuals retain the movement dependency. A word comparison as acceptance misses structure, targets and effects. Removing current-product checks would confuse a readable page with correct generation. Untracked or stale generated documents are unnecessary to batch native source work. A new authority, assignment allocator or proof archive supplies no reading judgment.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:final-conversion-reading-consequences`

Conversion errors can remain on the development line until final reading; that line is not release acceptance. Neither reader’s agreement is mathematical certification, and final review does not enlarge audit coverage. Admitted content batches need no separate per-transfer page or correspondence approval. Machinery, profiles and new native forms still receive ordinary independent review and their existing observations. M077, M082 and M083 are retired as active conversion requirements, with their numbers retained in the historical record. Their old equal/refused verdicts confer no new acceptance. Current source accounting and plan/output recovery are not retired with them. Source-coordinate annotations may still locate current occurrences but do not bind a transfer to its parent’s positions.

Generated artifacts stay committed and canonical under ADR 006/019: combine disjoint source changes, regenerate their shared outputs once, and check the retained batch. No source-only intermediate main state is thereby accepted. The reduced data-only round below changes regression timing, not the output byte check. Release retains ADR 019’s two independent productions. Protected conversion can precede renewal, but the nineteen final native passages retain exact-byte acceptance at release. The release goal and version boundary of ADR 030, signing, publication permission and the prohibition on self-acceptance remain.

**Amendment (Conversion verification)** · `amend:documents:final-conversion-reading`

These qualifications replace the conversion-specific procedure as a whole, including ADR 039’s Rule and its effective ADR 049 eligibility, capability, complete-conjunction and rerun obligations. The table quotes effective operative clauses; where an earlier file remains unchanged, the later replacement is cited. References to complete-gate eligibility and residual-only review do not survive as independent retention requirements. Other current-product, representation and release requirements remain. Earlier accepted files are not edited in place. ADR 039’s review-timing qualifications of ADRs 009, 017, 024, 026 and 005 now use this record’s complete final reading and current-product checks instead of mechanical eligibility and residual-only review; they do not require individual conversion-page review. Ordinary machinery review, theory approval and non-conversion obligations retain their existing scope.

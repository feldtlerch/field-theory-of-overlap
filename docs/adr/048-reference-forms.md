# ADR 048: Native reference forms and handle placements · `rec:adr:048-reference-forms`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:reference-forms`

A visible auxiliary paper handle is declared by a local selector and an exact canonical paper key in a statement unit's `documentary-targets` metadata, then placed once by `@handle{selector}` in its owning English paper paragraph. The generated paper retains the unstarred `\handle{key}` command at that position; Markdown and HTML show its plain unlinked spelling. The checked plan retains selector, key, spelling, position and paper origin. This form transfers a paper mint from an authored TeX source without admitting the target to the permanent theory or documentary registry.

Auxiliary paper mints have the distinct `UnitPaperMint` origin with their unit, declaration and placement sites. They join the global mint uniqueness check before the strict-unit projection removes paper origins. They can evidence an exact documentary target under M060, while they remain outside strict-unit and bare-label citation scope under M063. Documentary admission, if later made, retains the field, key and origin and derives status from ledger replay. A unit's primary id remains its own metadata mint.

Preparation accepts the generated `\handle` only inside a checked paragraph fragment, rejects duplicate generated auxiliary keys and rejects retained authored uses of a transferred key other than checked references. The final auxiliary check counts definitions before retaining values and requires one definition and a matching PDF destination for each transferred key. Source correspondence is independently reviewed as the ordered command, star and key tuples of the replaced parent interval and generated fragment; generated recovery alone does not establish that parent correspondence.

Ordinary paper tags retain their source key and command, and every reference-tag branch remains a plain unlinked handle on screen under ADR 037 (`rec:adr:037-native-paper-tagged-paragraphs`). An unstarred `@tag` in a converted paper paragraph must have bare print output, checked against the shipped identity and locator evidence. The existing located `J:occ` occurrence in `sec:log:andreoni-joint` is pinned by its unit bytes, marker identity and first paper-tag placement, and must remain located. A located unstarred occurrence requires the separately admitted @tag-located form and occurrence-level print-mode evidence; until then new located occurrences refuse.

The first consumer is the four-line tail after the equation in part F. Its marker replaces that tail without a new blank or paragraph token. The wrapper expands to the body alone, retaining the preceding prose and display in the paper; the screen presents the converted tail as a prose block with unavailable coverage for the preceding material. This record does not admit the partial passage to a content-only batch.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:reference-forms`

A handle can print a spelling and create a destination in one command. Retaining the command and its placement preserves the printed effect and the target while one metadata route owns the key. The global mint check catches duplicate ownership before a paper origin is excluded from strict citations. The auxiliary and PDF checks test executed definitions separately from the source and fragment checks.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:reference-forms-declined`

An automatic screen link is declined because no inbound screen link to these handles is admitted and ADR 037 keeps paper tags as plain handles. A second metadata field or inventory row for a transferred native mint is declined because it would make two owners for one key. A source-line pin for the legacy located tag is declined because unrelated lines may move its marker. An auxiliary number alone does not prove an occurrence's print mode.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:reference-forms-consequences`

The visible form requires document planning, all selected writers, their recovery, preparation and auxiliary counting together. The source interval's ordered commands and the paper and screen renderings require ordinary independent review under ADR 039 (`rec:adr:039-batch-conversion-review`): its Decision clause protects changed or lost claims and checks, while its capability clause reserves content-only batches for a fixed reviewed capability. ADR 016 (`rec:adr:016-admission-and-native-documents`) supplies the closed metadata route, and M060 supplies documentary target evidence. Forced-bare tags and silent handles wait for their consuming scopes; direct references wait for counter-context design, and new located tags wait for occurrence-level print-mode evidence. Counter-context work admits `@tag-located` with occurrence-level print-mode evidence before migrating its selector; it derives no ordinal for it. No such syntax is admitted here.

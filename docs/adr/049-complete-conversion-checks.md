# ADR 049: Complete conversion checks · `rec:adr:049-complete-conversion-checks`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:complete-conversion-checks`

A complete conversion check under ADR 039 (`rec:adr:039-batch-conversion-review`) compares the supplied parent and candidate under the parent's fixed reviewed capability, accepting repository history as presented without authenticating that history, the running program or a grant. Only assigned content and its prescribed derived outputs may change. Source, unit, structure, target, layout, mint, protected-passage, fresh-artifact, PDF-content and screen-recovery comparisons must all succeed, together with comparisons of checking inputs, effects and outcomes. Missing, stale, unsupported or different evidence refuses eligibility. Success concerns only this pair of commits; a changed parent requires another run.

Gate runs, refusals and dispositions are not archived, and reproducible evidence is not retained as a bundle. Assignments describe content scope, not authority, and require no allocator or sequence number. A correction is an ordinary change: unchanged compared content may meet ordinary eligibility; otherwise it takes ordinary review, with changes to a claim or check given to the project's owner. No separate correction baseline or record is used.

Final closed-list review receives the mechanically kept commits on which the gate can be rerun, the finished renderings and the remaining judgments named by ADR 039. Its human acceptance, independence and coverage record remains; the gate neither supplies nor authenticates it. Signing, publication, release permission and ordinary review of machinery retain their existing requirements.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:complete-conversion-checks-reasons`

Content correspondence and an unchanged-machinery boundary prevent a conversion from weakening its own checks without authenticating the system that invokes them. Fresh generation and complete proof coverage prevent submitted output from substituting for evidence. Recomputable results need no parallel archive. Human judgments and final-review coverage remain recorded because executing the gate cannot reconstruct them.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:complete-conversion-checks-declined`

A checker verifying its own history or binary mixes repository acceptance with comparison. External grants and allocation duplicate that boundary without checking another word or effect. Stored refusals cannot make a later run correct. A separate correction baseline could waive a difference from the actual parent.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:complete-conversion-checks-consequences`

A rerun may require fresh parent/candidate production. Unavailable pinned inputs cause an unavailable check, not a successful historical verdict. Selecting the actual parent remains part of using the repository, not an authenticated service. Partial checks still authorize no retention. Final review judges appearance, uncovered cross-target equivalence, truth of self-descriptions and stated residuals, not archived attempts or every source line.

ADR 039:11's “Assigned content and its prescribed derived outputs are not capability changes.” remains unchanged. Under its unchanged-source-blob rule, an unrelated base movement need not stale the assignment, but all checks run against the new actual parent. Whole-file closure still applies. The equality, display-body, no-machinery/test/shared-declaration/acceptance-field edits, source-accounting, protected-scope and release clauses at :13 remain. The discriminating artifact and missing-proof refusals at :27 remain prerequisites.

ADR 039:5's “with cumulative evidence available” remains unchanged: reproducible comparison evidence is available by rerunning the gate on the kept commits; human review evidence remains retained.

The other clauses at :25 govern ordinary changes and protected renewals; they do not create a conversion-run archive. Final-review independence still covers all content and machinery authors since the reference tag. Those human facts are recorded outside the gate, not supplied to it as a grant. Signing requirements elsewhere are untouched.

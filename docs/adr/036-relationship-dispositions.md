# ADR 036: Relationship dispositions at the complete-graph boundary · `rec:adr:036-relationship-dispositions`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:graph:relationship-dispositions`

At ADR 030's complete-graph boundary, each relationship of ADR 008's “earlier private implementation” (`dec:publication:fresh-history`) has exactly one recorded disposition: it is checked; it is replaced by an equivalent check whose record names what the old check established and how the new check establishes the same; or it carries an explicit unmigrated note. The checks performed by the inherited typesetting layer are relationships of the earlier system in this record's sense. An unmigrated note names the relationship and states either that it is debt, which stays visible in generated evidence until checked or replaced, or that the relationship is unwanted, with the reason. A reason that a relationship is unwanted states why the re-architected system should not hold it; a relationship that is merely hard to check is debt. An unmigrated note is not a check and is never counted or presented as one: no coverage figure, gate summary or document text includes it among checked relationships. A relationship with no disposition, a loss recorded without an unmigrated note, and a rule in a deferred state presented as a check are forbidden. Reproducing a verification that the earlier system performed belongs to the complete-graph boundary and takes one of the dispositions above; verifying anew what the earlier system only asserted is not required at the complete-graph boundary and belongs to ADR 030's native-model boundary, whose obligations this record does not decide. This classification does not suspend existing audit checks (`rec:adr:007-audit-on-the-engine`). The complete-document release gate is unchanged.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:graph:relationship-dispositions-reasons`

An explicit disposition lets the earlier system retire without silently losing a relationship. Equivalent checks preserve what was established even when the checking method changes. Visible debt and reasoned rejection distinguish unfinished checking from a relationship intentionally excluded from the re-architected system; neither can be reported as checked.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:graph:relationship-dispositions-declined`

Keeping a requirement to check every inherited relationship forces either a false equivalence or the retention of unwanted relationships. Allowing an unrecorded omission loses facts silently when the earlier system retires.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:graph:relationship-dispositions-consequences`

ADR 030 is amended without editing its accepted record (`conv:adr:form`). Its Decision sentence “The mathematics may be unverified, but it must be present, its links made, and the relationships the earlier system has must be checked.” becomes: “The mathematics may be unverified, but it must be present, its links made, and each relationship of the earlier system must have exactly one recorded disposition under ADR 036.” Its Reasons sentence “Faithful representation with preserved relationships permits retirement without claiming mathematical certification.” becomes: “Faithful representation with an explicit disposition for each relationship permits retirement without claiming mathematical certification.” Its Consequences sentence “A relationship is not checked by recording its loss or by registering a rule in a deferred state; permitting either at the complete-graph boundary would require superseding this decision.” becomes: “A relationship is not checked by recording its loss or by registering a rule in a deferred state; at the complete-graph boundary every relationship has exactly one recorded disposition: a check, an equivalent replacement check, or an unmigrated note that is never counted or presented as a check.” ADR 016:37's clause “residual authored-source machinery is retired only after equivalent native checks and independent acceptance exist” becomes: “residual authored-source machinery is retired only after each check it performs has a disposition under ADR 036 and independent acceptance exists”. The complete-document release gate and native-model boundary retain their meanings.

# ADR 014: The paper pair — authored LaTeX, reproducible build, checked addressing · `rec:adr:014-paper-pair`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:paper:authored-latex`

The complete English paper and its explainer are authored LaTeX: the 26 paths in `documents/paper-build/sources.tsv`, all under `documents/paper/`, are the pair's one source authority, and the build records that check them live beside it in `documents/paper-build/`. The pair is excepted from the statement-unit form and from its pre-delivery migration requirement (`rec:adr:005-statement-units`); it has no Markdown target, and strict-unit notation and language-weld rules do not apply to it, while converted units keep their rules. The pair is byte-gated (`rec:adr:006-byte-gated-generation`): one driver generates and checks both PDFs and their byte-input receipt.

The paper's typesetting layer is part of the paper's sources under the documents licence (`LICENSE-DOCS`): the macro files `documents/paper/macros_overlap.tex` and `documents/paper/econ_membrane.tex`, the typed-tools file `documents/paper/ftoo_typed_tools.tex`, the data tables `documents/paper/data/ftoo_manifest.tex`, `ftoo_registers.tex`, `ftoo_symbols.tex`, `ftoo_symbols_historical.tex` and `ftoo_index.tex`, the two document preambles `documents/paper/main.tex` and `documents/paper/main_explainer.tex.in`, and the explainer's setting files `documents/paper/explainer_strings_english.tex` and `documents/paper/explainer_displayskips.tex`. These files were not re-implemented in this repository. They are a declared and bounded exception to the fresh-history decision (`dec:publication:fresh-history`) that covers exactly these named files and nothing else: no crate, tool or build system enters with them, and the manifest table is the paper's typesetting export of the objects it names, which no code in this repository loads as a graph. The exception ends file by file, as each is replaced by generation from the theory graph or rewritten here.

The data tables are fixed data of this version: nothing in the repository regenerates them, and they are named `data` rather than `generated` because no generator owns them. Replacing them by generation from the theory graph is the stated next obligation of the paper pair; until then a change to them is an authored change, reviewed as text and surfaced by the byte receipt and the PDF byte gate.

The paper's object tags form a paper-scoped handle store, an exception to the two permanent stores (`rec:adr:003-tags-bound-to-labels`): the paper's own manifest data binds each of its 329 spellings, and three confirmed aliases print paper spellings. No paper-scoped spelling is admitted in either permanent registry. When the theory graph gains the node a paper tag names, the tag enters the theory registry unchanged and its paper row is removed in the same change. Documentary objects enter the documentary registry by confirmation (`dec:reader:confirmed-tags`); labels minted in the paper sources supply their target evidence through `documents/paper-build/label-mints.tsv`. Neither exception creates an identity domain or permits automatic promotion.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:paper:reasons`

- A complete, reproducibly built paper is checkable now: whole-pair reproduction keeps the argument, numbering, first-use state and appearance in one artifact a reader can open, while unit migration and graph coverage proceed independently and family by family.
- Byte equality makes reproduction checkable by anyone with the pinned support, and the tag lookup keeps every printed handle resolvable to a label the paper defines.
- The typesetting layer is typesetting source under the documents licence, not program code; like everything this repository publishes it is subject to the publication audit (`rec:policy:publication-audit`), its executable strings and rendered output included, and re-implementing it before the graph can generate its tables would be a new renderer with visual-regression risk and no gain in checked content. Its executable checks run inside this bounded layer; no new semantic checker or claim of checking coverage is added to it.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:paper:declined`

Migrating the prose to statement units before delivery couples delivery to the full conversion. Generating the typed layer and data tables from the theory graph now is not yet possible, because the graph does not carry these objects. Re-implementing the typesetting layer by hand before that generation exists replaces one hand-maintained layer with another. Admitting the paper's tags as documentary handles would misclass theory objects, and admitting them to the theory registry needs graph nodes that do not exist yet.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:paper:consequences`

The typesetting layer remains executable machinery with a stated replacement obligation. The paper's bibliography backend is BibTeX in both documents, and two error messages in the macros describe local conditions and the actions available in this repository. Nineteen passages in which the paper and explainer describe their own construction and checking are bound to accepted bytes in `documents/paper-build/self-description.json`; the companion's front matter states that its tags resolve into the paper's label space at every build. The typed layer audits authored grades at result ends and section boundaries: necessary results refuse any recorded spend, and anchored-contingent results refuse held or open spends. Other grades are not refused by the grade ceiling; an open spend warns. The machinery rejects undeclared spends, forbidden branches and unknown evidence keys, and renders the constants ledger from those same declarations. Dimension equality is checked only within `\CheckedSum`, which the paper does not call; there is no separate typed comparison command. It does not compute grades, check untyped prose or legacy displays, certify declarations' truth or regenerate registers from a live theory graph. Each passage has an anchor, byte length, final-byte hash and filled reading acceptance identifying the accepted bytes. The paper gate locates each accepted passage exactly once by anchor and hash and requires a filled acceptance. A later change to those bytes invalidates its acceptance; unrelated offset shifts do not. Every other compatibility substitution belongs to the preloaded adapter.

The embedded bibliography backend does not implement `uniquename` or `uniquelist`. Six brace-protected short-author fields in the derived database supply the initials the backend's missing `uniquename` would, without changing author fields or the printed reference list; the single accent normalization works around a pinned engine defect in `text.length$`, and the bundle's biblatex style is used unchanged. Every database or derivation change requires reviewed input and output hashes. Byte equality establishes reproduction, not theory validity or full equivalence to another bibliography processor.

`python3 -B tools/paper/prepare.py REPOSITORY SUPPORT EMPTY_DIRECTORY` checks the closed source list, the layout pins and the pinned package and font bytes, instantiates templates and derives the bibliography in disposable storage. The runtime record refers to the shared support lock. `tools/paper/bibroute.py retirement` runs two independent bounded probes: the accent probe determines whether the remaining engine workaround can be retired, and the integer probe checks that the pinned engine still accepts the entry-local integer assignment the stock style relies on. Delivered PDF bytes and their reproducible build are governed by the artifact gate; the release rule M065 is evaluated live by `paper check --release`, and page acceptance and publication remain separate human steps outside the repository.

Local layout deviations preserve the source words, formal expressions and reference spellings. The shapes register in `documents/paper/parts/appendices.tex` uses flexible-column weights totaling two, so its content fits inside the page and its footer remains clear. The rests-table hook in `documents/paper-build/reader-hooks.tex` uses 4 pt column padding to keep its role-column line breaks and the footer on the page. The adapter places the six affected box and item destinations with their labels and emits each long-table destination once. Named companion links keep their destination names without an unused numeric page option. The warning sign uses a triangle and exclamation mark in the existing face family; a discretionary break in the derived bibliography title keeps its letters.

The reader hooks enumerate the paragraphs that need extra line-breaking stretch and restore the prior setting afterward; paragraphs ending in a display receive the same setting inside that display's group. Three alignment displays use local minimum separations, and one definition uses a two-point reduction after its grade. Index locators keep their reference type's separator; the index location column uses 0.14 of the text width so appendix locators fit without stranded letters. Every line-addressed hook has a pin in `documents/paper-build/layout-pins.toml` naming its target file, lines and the file's hash; preparation refuses a hook address without a pin, a pin without a hook address, and a changed target, naming the hook before typesetting. These repairs change neither the document-wide measure and spacing nor the accepted self-description passages.

The raw-identity pattern puts the literal hyphen first in its character class, keeping its matches without an invalid-range warning. Every auxiliary/PDF destination discrepancy and every missing, surplus or clipped visible occurrence refuses without a baseline exception.

The seal theorem starts its introduction below its long heading to avoid crowded sentence spacing or an isolated final word. The legacy-symbol paragraph shares line stretch with a local one-em right-margin allowance, reducing loose word spacing; the prior margin setting is restored after the paragraph.

The remaining page repairs are line-addressed and pinned in the same way, each confined to its site and restored afterward. One run-in heading begins a new page when less than five lines' height remains on the page at that point, instead of standing at a page foot with one line of its item; the test is made as the page is built, so the break follows earlier pages when they move and forces no break otherwise. One box's closing paragraph may not break a page before its last line, and another's page break may not follow a hyphenated line. Four paragraphs are set without hyphens, with the extra stretch that needs: two so that a locator keeps its reference name whole, and two so that no hyphenated word is carried across a page break. Three paragraphs keep at least two words and no word fragment on their last line.

Economics and statement box titles are set flush left so that a wrapped title's first line keeps natural word spacing; unlike the line-addressed repairs, this style applies to every such box in both documents, and only titles that wrap change. The concordance and equivalence captions take the full measure so that each fits one line. The explainer's formal notes end their paragraph inside their size group, so all of them keep their own footnote-size line spacing; this is a document-wide change in the explainer's look.

The companion's page locator suppresses the reference command's own name because its caller supplies the page abbreviation. Table references name their table kind: the constants, rulings, branches, equivalences and evidence tables supply their object kind to the reference machinery, and constants-ledger row anchors carry it too, so references to them and to their rows name the table rather than the surrounding appendix. Four-point column padding in the constants ledger gives the following empty rulings table room to close above the page foot; a local no-break keeps it attached to its introducing paragraph.

Evidence labels in the bibliography notes use linked reader tags, keeping every other word and the existing bibliography punctuation. The verification table keeps “itself” whole with a word-specific hyphenation exception; the permitted break is restored when that table ends.

# ADR 043: Source-decided line-end comparison · `rec:adr:043-source-decided-line-ends`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:paper:source-decided-line-ends`

Complete-document comparison interprets a line-end mark from the captured source of that occurrence on each side independently. A source-confirmed discretionary hyphen is absent from the canonical reader tokens; a lexical hyphen, dash, slash and authored token separation remain. An unchanged event can compare without interpretation only on the evidence below. Raw pages and marks are never discarded from the evidence. Source correspondence determines only these local interpretations, not which document text is compared. Unknown line-end interpretation remains coverage debt; absent or ambiguous selected-paragraph correspondence is a fatal failure. After fatal failures have been excluded, a difference independent of unresolved events reports different. Otherwise a symmetric deletion witness may prove unequal streams, giving different with that coverage recorded; unresolved events with no such proof give refused, never equal. Equal canonical tokens are evidence of preserved extracted content, not page acceptance.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:paper:source-decided-line-ends-reasons`

A line ending is a typesetting choice. The same reader word may appear joined, split at different letters, or split on only one side. A raw position cannot establish whether a mark belongs to that word or whether a following space was authored. Independent source correspondence distinguishes these cases without a dictionary, general punctuation removal or a parent spelling imposed on a candidate. Comparing complete streams still exposes additions outside a selected passage. A conservative unchanged-residual proof permits already identical legacy material to remain uninterpreted without claiming a paragraph identity that has not been established. Every admitted interpretation changes only the four event marks and whitespace, so erasing those characters everywhere from both streams produces the same witness before or after any interpretation. Unequal witnesses therefore prove that no choice of event interpretations can make the streams equal; equal witnesses prove nothing and never authorize equality.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:paper:source-decided-line-ends-declined`

Deleting every line-end hyphen merges lexical compounds and can hide a changed assertion. Joining by a word list does not establish what the source says. Equal raw offsets move when earlier content reflows; equal extracted strings can conceal different source separators. Requiring all legacy mathematics and prose to be decoded before any reflow can compare equal couples unrelated conversion work. A residual proof based only on equal PDF text is declined: it also needs unchanged enclosing source evidence and identical raw event lines. Unsupported events cannot establish equality; this is not arbitrary TeX interpretation. Deleting only the event-local marks on each side is not sufficient: a lexical hyphen at a line end on one side and mid-line on the other would receive asymmetric treatment.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:paper:source-decided-line-ends-consequences`

The residual proof relies on same-input reproduction and a complete captured input envelope, not an assertion that receipt hashes alone prove how a PDF was built. A residual is proved whole: a changed locator, moved float or footnote, a converted unit in another source outside the selected set, or an unaccounted envelope change invalidates its bypass for every event. Such a comparison cannot be equal until admitted normalization or located source coverage resolves that debt; a deletion witness may still establish different. Capturing both source inventories, validating inclusion substitutions and retaining token provenance cost work and evidence space, and a single-unit comparison cannot establish a multi-unit conversion return. For an authored-parent selector, any other edit in the selected source is a selector failure, not residual coverage.

**Rule (Source-decided reflow)** · `rule:documents:source-decided-reflow`

An event is a raw extracted line ending in U+002D, U+2013, U+2014 or U+002F, allowing horizontal whitespace before its newline, or the corresponding source occurrence whose mark is not at a line end on the other side. Page-end events use the retained page-boundary separator. An unchanged event bypasses interpretation only when its raw line, aligned canonical-token position and enclosing captured source bytes are identical. The position is paragraph identity and token ordinal within that paragraph, never physical page, line number or character offset. Every other event requires unique correspondence to its own side's source of each selected occurrence, at its checked inclusion place. Canonical tokens preserve decoded letters, digits, punctuation, tag identity and authored separation; only source-confirmed discretionary breaks disappear. Evidence records the occurrence, both raw and source locations, interpretation or bypass proof, and the complete comparison. Unknown identity, unsupported syntax or ambiguous matching refuses the affected proof. Unproved line-end events remain coverage debt; only the negative witness below can establish different without resolving them, and none can establish equal.

Until a legacy interval has paragraph identities, it may instead use an unchanged-residual proof: the ordered interval between mapped paragraphs has identical compared text after every admitted normalization, every event on either side has exactly one counterpart with the same residual key and an identical raw line, and the entire captured source envelope outside those mapped paragraphs is byte-identical after one checked native-inclusion substitution per selected occurrence. Its stable key is document, residual-interval ordinal and mark-retaining token ordinal within that interval. All three facts are required. The envelope includes source lists, source files, fragments, tag inputs, macros, profile/settings and support identities. Each checked substitution also admits the selected occurrence's inclusion row and exactly the marker substitution's implied line shift for later rows of the same source; all other row fields remain unchanged. Each selected row sits in generator order: paper occurrences before explainer occurrences, then closed source-list order and marker line within each document. Receipt input rows are verified against captured bytes and compared row by row after those substitutions, not by the receipt's overall digest. An unavailable or otherwise changed envelope refuses this fallback. This is an explicit bounded alternative to paragraph identification, not evidence that a residual interval is a paragraph. A shifted character offset alone invalidates neither proof.

The negative witness deletes U+002D, U+2013, U+2014, U+002F and all whitespace from both complete compared streams, including counterparts that are not at a line end. It is used only to prove different when line-end coverage is unresolved, never as an equality comparison or a normalization. Selector, capture, extraction, selected-paragraph decoding, correspondence or bounds failures refuse before any difference verdict. A difference that holds without the unresolved events (bookmark titles, source/body or source/tags, or a document stream with no unresolved events) reports different. The witness decides only otherwise. This witness applies only to otherwise valid comparisons with unresolved line-end coverage; it conservatively erases all four marks pending their real extraction controls, although hyphen and whitespace deletion alone would be sound.

**Amendment (Record and rule text)** · `amend:documents:source-decided-line-end-amendments`

The unchanged sentence “The same mark at the same line-end position on both sides does not establish whether a hyphen is lexical or discretionary or whether a following space was authored.” remains true: the proofs require more than that position. ADR 038's raw evidence, furniture, scalar and ligature limits, checked-tag case limitation, extractor capture, three verdicts and independent acceptance remain. Same-input reproduction and generated-artifact checks are unchanged. Locator digits and floated/table content receive no new exclusion.

M077 remains Enforced with no new rule number. Its full replacement statement is: “Requested paper-pair comparisons read captured PDFs in full through the recorded extractor, retain every raw page and classify every character under the stated profile and normalization rules. They compare complete canonical document streams and ordered bookmark titles and corroborate the declared paragraph with each side's captured source of that occurrence at its checked inclusion place. Only verified folios, whitespace, the listed presentation ligatures and source-decided line-end interpretation change extracted text. Line-end hyphens, en dashes, em dashes and slashes are retained as events before interpretation. An unchanged event may bypass interpretation only with an identical raw line, the same aligned token position and identical enclosing source bytes; an unidentified legacy interval may use the stricter unchanged-residual proof stated in ADR 043, never an offset-only match. Every other event requires unique source correspondence on each side independently; only a source-confirmed discretionary hyphen is removed, and authored marks and separators are retained. Complete comparison includes all text outside the corroborated paragraph. Checked bare paper tags retain the measured extracted small-cap spelling without general case folding, and correspondence refuses a mismatch; decoded paragraph text also compares their semantic spellings and targets. Outside decoded paragraph text, case inside small capitals is compared as extracted, so a source case change there can compare equal. Invalid or changed inputs, unavailable or ambiguous extraction, unsupported profiles or syntax, invalid furniture, absent or ambiguous selected-paragraph correspondence and exceeded correspondence bounds refuse. Unequal canonical content or bookmark titles reports differences. A difference that holds without the unresolved events (bookmark titles, source/body or source/tags, or a document stream with no unresolved events) reports different. The witness decides only otherwise. If only unresolved line-end coverage prevents complete interpretation, deleting the four event marks and whitespace everywhere from both streams may prove different when the resulting witnesses are unequal; otherwise the verdict is refused. Unresolved events are retained as coverage and never authorize equal. Changed locators, reflow at other break points and moved floats remain stated coverage limits, not permission to erase content, and can report differences without changed words. Equality attests only these comparisons, not paragraph boundaries, mathematical structure, checking effects, appearance or acceptance.”

Its replacement scope is: “Explicit complete-paper-and-explainer comparison requests using the A4 plain-folio profile, a declared plain paragraph with checked bare paper tags and the bounded source-decided line-end grammar; complete raw and canonical streams, ordered bookmark titles, per-side captured source and unchanged-event/residual evidence. No automatic release or conversion acceptance, no derived-locator exclusion and no general legacy TeX decoder.”

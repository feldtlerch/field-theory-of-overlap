# ADR 055: Bounded corpus-slot composition · `rec:adr:055-bounded-corpus-slot`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:bounded-corpus-slot`

A declared source interval may transfer to one coarse native unit containing an ordered heading, paragraphs and checked uses of existing native units. Its distinct corpus-slot composition replaces one unique marker line. Complete source accounting, exactly one occurrence of every existing inclusion, preservation of inherited mints and checking effects, and emission and recovery for every selected kind apply to the entire interval. The initial admission is an unnumbered section heading and paragraphs with decoded text, strong and emphasis runs and named en-space intent. Other kinds refuse. Existing statement-unit and one-line fragment contracts remain unchanged.

Each selected child corresponds to its own source on each side. The inherited side uses the source-bound importer projection; the native side uses its own checked native data. The selected slot's complete ordered content, including existing inclusions, is matched without omitted or reordered interior material. Independent comparison covers both complete PDFs and compares the selected recovered screens with the selected new PDF. En-space intent emits its paper space and one ASCII separator in each screen. This is an excerpt admission, not complete-document coverage or unattended partial-file retention.

An unchanged legacy residual may surround one checked ordered corpus slot under the same three facts as ADR 043: identical compared residual text, a bijection of residual events with identical raw lines, and a byte-identical outside input envelope after the checked inclusion substitution. Child proofs establish the slot's complete content before it supplies a residual boundary. Only the specified inclusion-row and fragment-input substitutions are admitted; receipt rows remain compared individually. Missing correspondence or any other changed envelope refuses this fallback.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:bounded-corpus-slot-reasons`

One coarse unit can preserve ordered structure without assigning a handwritten identity to every paragraph. Sharing the inherited decoder avoids divergent interpretations of the same TeX while independent source binding prevents a candidate from supplying its parent's words. Matching every child and the complete PDF streams catches omission that a slot boundary alone cannot see. The residual proof remains conservative outside the selected interval.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:bounded-corpus-slot-declined`

Raw interval replay is not native content. A multiline body disguised as a paragraph fragment evades that route's contract. Two independently widened inherited grammars duplicate work without supplying independent PDF evidence. A parent export used for both sides can hide a native edit. Ignoring inner paragraphs, receipt changes or other source inputs would weaken the residual proof. The existing Python decoder remains available for its narrower existing consumers.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:bounded-corpus-slot-consequences`

Located runs and checked composition add interfaces to the existing owners. Recovered screens provide independent output evidence, not independent authorship of the source. The selected interval may still refuse when residual content reflows beyond its source coverage; no locator, float or legacy-mathematics exclusion is added. Ordinary independent review applies to this machinery and consumer. Eligible unattended corpus transfers still require whole-file closure and the complete retention gate. En-space appearance on screens differs from paper without changing its words.

# ADR 026: Warranted structural change · `rec:adr:026-warranted-structural-change`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:graph:warranted-change`

Structural changes are assessed by their effect on meaning and historical references, with greater conceptual warrant required for deeper effects.

**Rule (warranted change)** · `rule:graph:warranted-change`

A well-motivated change is admissible in every layer; its required warrant follows its effect, not its line count. Identity ordinarily keeps naming the same thing and public targets keep resolving because readers and retained evidence depend on them. A change of classification is not permission to change the meaning of an old citation silently. Depth determines the argument, not the approver. ADR 016 reserves theory approval and excludes self-acceptance (`rec:adr:016-admission-and-native-documents`); ADR 024 governs who accepts changes (`rec:adr:024-paper-layout-without-coordinates`).

| Depth by effect | What changes | Required re-check | Warrant and retained record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Document reading order or grouping | Placement and layout, with the same referents | Inventory, destinations, numbers, first mentions and all selected renderings | Reviewed commit with reason, projection diff and bounded page/screen evidence. |
| 2 · Document reading kind | Current structural treatment, with the same referent | Allowed children/relations, body accounting, addresses, numbering scopes and writer treatment | Reviewed commit naming old/new treatment and reason; a decision record if a contract changes. |
| 3 · Document reading merge or split | Presentation of existing material, without changing what old references mean | Complete body correspondence, retained targets and changed grouping | Reviewed commit and explicit old/new correspondence; if a reference changes meaning, use depth 7. |
| 4 · Theory payload restatement | Wording or representation of the same value/proposition | Admission, notation, dependencies and stale evidence; regenerate bound evidence | Reviewed mathematical reason and full payload/input diff; a changed thesis is depth 7, not a restatement. |
| 5 · Audit role | What an evidence node attests or how it is accepted | All evidence consumers, route isolation, acceptance, historical/current interpretation | Decision record with conceptual case, why a successor or shallower treatment is inadequate if rejected, and evidence migration. |
| 6 · Theory reclassification preserving referent, if future kinds permit it | Classification, typed label, tag resolution, notation and audit selection | Every consumer of the changed kind and all historical/current references | Decision record choosing the label route and explaining why any rejected successor or shallower treatment fails; retain correspondence and evidence history. |
| 7 · Changed referent or reference meaning | Theory merges/splits, changed thesis, quantity-to-claim or claim-to-quantity change, or changed identity/target interpretation | Version-explicit meaning of every old reference, all dependent claims, notation, selection, evidence and renderings | Strongest decision record: old/new referents, why the chosen change is necessary, why a shallower change is inadequate, and why any rejected successor route is inadequate; no silent reassignment. |

For the deepest-depth test ask: would a reader of an old citing text now read it as naming a different object or proposition? If yes, the change is depth 7 even when only one word changes. Printed numbers are derived locators, not identities: renumbering caused by insertion or reordering is depth 1, and an old text citing only a printed number is not an old reference for the depth-7 test, which concerns identities, labels, tags and destinations. Quantity and Claim are the two current theory kinds; one denotes a value and the other a proposition, so every current change between them is depth 7. A theory merge or split is also depth 7. Rephrasing the same proposition may be depth 4, but it still invalidates evidence bound to its former wording.

The document-reading rows concern the paper's or explainer's structure, whereas a change to a field-anchored or latent theory reading is a theory change classified by its effect at depths 4–7. Addressing and tag changes, and changes to the validation-rule register, take the depth of their effect on meaning or historical references; their location outside a document reading or theory payload does not exempt them from this table. Each structural change of a kind in this table states its depth and reason in the commit body; at depths 5–7 a decision record also states them. Review refuses a change that understates its effect; at depth 5 or deeper lacks a decision record considering the successor and shallower alternatives; leaves any old reference without a stated resolution or treats evidence of an old statement as current for a changed one; or omits a consumer of the changed fact from revalidation. Retiring an object with its reason and admitting a corrected object under a new identity uses ADR 001's ordinary successor route and requires no decision record under this record, regardless of the modelling error's depth; the depth-5–7 record requirement applies when a change keeps an identity, label or public target while changing its referent, classification or attestation. A persistent-identity route must explain specifically why a successor does not suffice and prove unambiguous historical resolution; inability to distinguish old and new uses of the same unversioned name refuses that route.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:graph:warranted-change-reasons`

A closed model makes its present classification checkable, not infallible. Structural improvement needs a path that increases conceptual accountability with semantic depth. Preserving old references and the exact statements checked by retained evidence makes correction possible without rewriting earlier meanings.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:graph:warranted-change-declined`

Permanent prohibition preserves modelling mistakes. Unqualified mutation silently changes citations and evidence. Counting edited lines mistakes small semantic changes for harmless ones. A machine rule claiming to judge conceptual warrant would certify something its validator cannot establish.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:graph:warranted-change-consequences`

ADR 001:20 says: “A wrongly modeled object is never edited into a different object: it retires with its reason recorded and a corrected object enters with a new identity, so old texts and retained audit reports keep meaning what they meant.” Its successor mechanism remains valid and needs no supersession when used. Replace that whole quoted sentence with this complete qualified wording: “A wrongly modeled object ordinarily retires with its reason recorded and a corrected object enters with a new identity. A warranted persistent-identity change requires a decision record naming the old and new referents, why a successor or shallower change is inadequate, and an unambiguous historical resolution: an unversioned reference made before such a change resolves to the meaning it had when made, and retained evidence continues to attest only its recorded statement and inputs. On either path, old texts and retained audit reports keep meaning what they meant.” The replacement retains the requirement that old texts and retained audit reports keep their meanings. The permission does not implement historical resolution or waive any current admission check. ADR 001:5's closed sets and M005/M006 continue to validate each current revision; no review permission is inserted into those machine statements. A persistent-identity change that alters a label also affects ADR 001:5's stable-semantic-name clause, ADR 003:5 and :11's permanent semantic-label binding and no-rebinding clauses, ADR 016:5 and :31's preserved-binding clauses, and the tag-history and resolution rules M030, M032–M034 and M036; a decision record choosing that route must quote and supersede the affected ADR clauses, update the affected tag rules, and carry tag-history and validation migration in the same change. ADR 026 itself supersedes none of those clauses or tag rules.

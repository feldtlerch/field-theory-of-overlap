# ADR 016: Admission and native document renderings · `rec:adr:016-admission-and-native-documents`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:admission-and-native-renderings`

A distinction between theory objects that carries a rule keeps its own kind, with no target number of kinds; a new node kind still enters only on two members or a demonstrated structural need (`rec:adr:001-closed-typed-graph`). An admitted object remains addressable independently of audit coverage, with audit status shown separately and retirement retaining its distinct meaning. Permanent reader handles are required where readers actually address objects; existing admissions and their bindings are preserved, and internal objects without reader uses need no handle. Evidence warrants are distinct link records with their existing handles, not mathematical premises; recording a warrant does not establish its evidence, and this decision specifies no new mathematical checking route (`rec:adr:007-audit-on-the-engine`).

Ordinary unit ids retain the lowercase three-component label grammar; a separate, closed documentary-target field in unit metadata may mint exact admitted documentary targets whose names do not fit that grammar, preserving their reader handles and destinations as authored sources are replaced. This uses the unit-metadata mint the tag decision already admits (`rec:adr:003-tags-bound-to-labels`) and widens neither the grammar of ordinary unit ids nor that of authority-record labels. Candidate target mints are collected before documentary-ledger replay, so membership is not justified circularly by the handle being admitted. Admission refuses wrong case or kind, duplicate mints and rebinding; retained bindings require native target evidence even after handle retirement.

Routine page and screen review may be appointed, approval of theory changes stays with the project's owner, every bound self-description passage is to carry a named renewal acceptor, and the author of a change never accepts it. Appointments belong to acceptance evidence, not this record. A change to accepted passage bytes requires renewed acceptance; an offset shift alone does not invalidate unchanged accepted bytes (`rec:adr:014-paper-pair`).

The paper and explainer are to be generated natively from one source into PDF, Markdown and HTML, with no rendering corrected by hand and the reader rules holding in every rendering; complete native documents in all three media gate the pair's release 0.6.0 (whose development version is set as `0.6.0-dev` in `documents/paper-build/templates.toml`), and the full mathematical model beyond the present claim audit gates release 0.7.0. All native-document conversion and replacement checking belong to release 0.6.0: no authored source file covered by ADR 014's source authority survives that release, and its temporary exception machinery is removed once native checks replace it. The PDF remains a rendering; HTML is one page per document, with an internal contents list and reciprocal links between the documents. Release 0.6.0 does not enlarge the audit's scope, and every rendering states what is and is not audited, independently of document completeness (`rec:adr:002-status-and-coverage`). The obligations the full model must meet and the routes by which its evidence is checked belong to that release's own decision; this record does not decide them.

During conversion, a generated fragment may replace a declared, bounded part of an authored source file, and a unit that belongs to exactly one skeleton may be quoted in the other document by a typed quotation-use without a second mint or authored copy, while the old checks remain active on every still-unconverted source. A quotation-use names the quoted unit, renders its shared text and links to its destination in the document whose skeleton contains it; it places the unit in no second skeleton, so each unit stays in exactly one skeleton (`rec:adr:005-statement-units`). Where a converted passage appears in both documents, both appearances are replaced in the same change and its target mint transfers atomically, and generated fragments enter only through declared parts checked by the existing paper preparation and document generation checks. A rendering made before conversion is complete cannot satisfy the complete-document release gate. Converted units retain their notation and language-weld checks, and every generated fragment and rendering retains shared generation/check and byte equality (`rec:adr:006-byte-gated-generation`).

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:admission-and-native-reasons`

- Kinds express constraints, so a numerical quota would erase distinctions that validation needs or create distinctions with no rule to justify them.
- Addressing identifies what a reader is discussing; audit coverage states what evidence has been checked. Coupling them would prevent an honest account of an admitted but unaudited object, while using retirement for missing evidence would falsely withdraw its identity.
- Permanent handles preserve references readers already use. Requiring handles for every internal object adds names without a reader need; treating warrants as premises instead would confuse evidence links with the mathematical assumptions of a claim.
- Closed documentary targets preserve printed names and destinations without weakening ordinary unit identity. Collecting mint evidence before replay gives the permanent ledger an independent target to validate, including for retired handles.
- Independent acceptance separates reproduction from judgment: a byte gate proves reproducibility, while theory approval, page and screen review, and passage renewal assess different claims.
- One source prevents the words and destinations in different renderings from drifting. Separate document and full-model release gates make complete reading surfaces possible without implying that more mathematics has been checked.
- Incremental fragments and typed quotation let both documents consume one statement while preserving the checks that still protect authored material; a second copy would recreate the drift the unit form removes.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:admission-and-native-declined`

A fixed kind count discards rule-bearing distinctions. Restricting reader addresses to audit coverage hides unresolved objects. Naming every internal object creates permanent editorial obligations without a reader use. Recasting warrants as premises changes the mathematics. Widening every unit id to arbitrary documentary names loses the closed identity grammar. Separately corrected renderings and duplicated quotations introduce competing sources. Requiring the full mathematical audit before native document delivery couples independent evidence obligations; removing old checks before their native replacements exist leaves the unconverted sources unchecked.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:admission-and-native-consequences`

This record partially supersedes ADR 003's Decision clause “the theory registry covers every permanent theory identity” with handle coverage for actual reader addressing, preserving every existing admission. It also supersedes the Decision's out-of-slice state as an addressing refusal — “Theory tags carry live, retired and out-of-slice states” and the out-of-slice store in “The generated runtime keeps retired and out-of-slice tags in separate stores with separate messages” — so audit exclusion alone cannot make an admitted object unaddressable, while absence and retirement keep their own refusals. Permanent binding, terminal retirement, exact unknown/retired refusals, unique live handles at reader citations and the existing Markdown label-comment policy remain (`rec:adr:003-tags-bound-to-labels`).

This record partially supersedes ADR 014's first Decision paragraph: “it has no Markdown target” gives way to the three-rendering release contract, and the clauses making “the 26 paths in `documents/paper-build/sources.tsv`” the pair's “one source authority” and excepting the pair “from the statement-unit form and from its pre-delivery migration requirement” now hold only for the authored sources not yet converted, ending as native generation replaces them; ADR 015's authored debts table therefore stays under that exception until its appendix is converted. The strict-unit notation and language-weld exception in that paragraph likewise remains bounded to still-unconverted sources; converted units keep their rules. ADR 014's same-change transfer of an unchanged paper handle into the theory registry with removal of its paper row is retained, as are the source-list, layout-pin, support-pin, accepted-passage and PDF/receipt byte checks. Its bounded typesetting exception, its paper-scoped handle store and the label-mint inventory evidence remain only for the still-unconverted portion and end file by file as native generation and checking replace their subjects; no new exception is admitted (`rec:adr:014-paper-pair`). No other decision record is superseded: the per-target display bodies of the statement-unit decision and the label comments of the tag decision stand until a later decision names the clause it changes.

The addressing code of the manifest crate must revise M035 to classify address availability independently of audit coverage and M036 to require handles for reader-addressed objects while preserving all admissions and exact lifecycle refusals. Unit admission, the joint mint inventory and the documentary registry must implement the closed documentary-target contract under M060; the paper-inventory target alternative remains temporary until native evidence replaces every retained target. These are intended scopes for later implementation, not claims of enforcement or changes to registered rule states (`rec:adr:010-validation-rules`). Tests at those consumers must cover empty and partial audit slices, unhandled internal objects, exact case/kind, duplicate mint, rebinding and retained retired-target evidence.

Document assembly and emission must check single skeleton membership and typed quotation, and paper preparation must check declared generated parts, source removal, pins and unchanged accepted bytes together. The paper-scoped store and M060's paper-inventory alternative are retired only after native bindings and targets cover their retained obligations, and residual authored-source machinery is retired only after equivalent native checks and independent acceptance exist. These obligations do not enlarge audit coverage, admit theory data or alter reader artifacts merely by being recorded.

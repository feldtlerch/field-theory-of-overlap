# ADR 039: Mechanical conversion and final residual review · `rec:adr:039-batch-conversion-review`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:batch-conversion-review`

Eligible native conversion batches publish to the main line when their complete mechanical gate passes; individual or sampled review is not required, and individual review is permitted. Changed line and page breaks with unchanged content are not defects and are not checked per batch; major page movements are judged once after conversion and adjustment. Until locator and whole-stream hyphenation capabilities exist, a batch whose reflow changes a printed locator or a line-end hyphen is not retained; this is a comparison-coverage limit, not a content defect. The independent review before release judges only appearance, cross-target equivalence where no check covers it, the truth of self-descriptions and the stated residuals of mechanical comparison over the complete rendered result, with cumulative evidence available; it does not include a full rereading of converted source. Every reviewer under this record has a different model family from every author of the reviewed work, including every machinery author since the reference tag for the final review. Machinery, rules, records, gates and capability changes retain ordinary independent review before retention. A changed, qualified or lost claim or check stops its scope and requires the project's owner's review before it lands. Reproduction and generated-artifact byte checks remain required. Main-line publication means retention of a gated batch with its regenerated artifacts on the main development line; release publication, page acceptance and publication permission remain the separate human steps of ADR 014:29 and ADR 024:21, after final acceptance and at the release tag.

The version remains 0.6.0-dev until release. Numbered development tags v0.6.0-dev.1, v0.6.0-dev.2 and successors identify fixed reference states when needed; v0.6.0-dev.2 precedes any further native conversion of reader content, including reviewed capability consumers, and is the word and appearance/page-movement reference for final review; it incorporates the qualification of checking descriptions recorded in registry/paper-layout/self-description-wording-renewal.md since v0.6.0-dev.1. It already includes a native paragraph whose PDFs were byte-identical to its parent. The release tag v0.6.0 identifies the independently reviewed state; patch re-releases are v0.6.1 and successors.

**Rule (batch conversion evidence)** · `rule:documents:batch-conversion-evidence`

Eligible native conversion changes only assigned content through a fixed reviewed capability, its fixed path classes and declaration template, with freshly derived artifacts and composition. Complete conversion proposals receive retention sequence numbers on arrival and are separately re-gated against their actual parents; a refused proposal retains its evidence without blocking other complete returns. Under a fixed capability only changed assigned source blobs stale a changed base, and actual-parent evidence replaces earlier comparison evidence. The capability binds trusted code, admission grammar, notation definitions and primitive maps, rendering profiles/templates, public bindings, support/toolchain pins, checker configuration and rule contract; changes to any of these, including additive definitions, invalidate the capability. Assigned content and its prescribed derived outputs are not capability changes. Each whole-file assignment requires its complete capability closure before conversion.

The complete gate requires equal reader words, digits, punctuation, formulas, tags, styles, cells, citations, target identities, ordered bookmark titles, checking inputs and effects, and structural meaning: kinds, containment, order and numbering events. Every display target body is compared with its parent content. No authored machinery, rule, test, shared declaration or acceptance-field edit is eligible. Unknown forms, unmet checks, unassigned edits, source overlap, changed capabilities, stale or incomplete evidence, any compared difference or lost check refuse eligibility. Every changed source span is accounted for; reproduction, shared text and destinations, layout attachment, mint checks and protected overlap checks remain required. Retention-gate refusals and dispositions are retained; a disposition identifies evidenced locator or hyphenation differences without waiving them. Evidence records author and reviewer designations with model families and reviewed coverage; acceptance under this record requires family independence from every author of the reviewed work. For final review this is the union of authors of the complete converted result and every machinery author since the reference tag, including when review coverage is divided by part. Protected scopes remain ineligible until separate structured-selection and transfer authority permits them. Mechanical success authorizes eligible retention with regenerated artifacts on the main development line, not release publication, page acceptance or publication permission. Final human acceptance covers the complete rendered result's closed residual judgments and precedes the release tag. A permitted individual review's finding is handled as a defect report against retained content: a correction passes the same gate, or requires owner review if it concerns a claim or check; the review does not retrospectively make that batch ineligible.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:batch-conversion-review-reasons`

Exact content correspondence permits disjoint conversion without repeated judgments about temporary page positions. Each target's parent comparison protects values that the PDF cannot see. The final rendered result is the coherent subject for the judgments the gate cannot make. Restricting the exception to unchanged reviewed capabilities prevents a conversion from weakening its own evidence.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:batch-conversion-review-declined`

ADR 009 declines a large prepared import because “review at that scale is skimming with ceremony”. A final rereading of every source line would have that same weakness. Here the cumulative mechanical correspondence replaces that task, while explicit residual judgments cover the complete renderings; divided coverage must remain exhaustive. This concentrates judgment but does not prove the machinery infallible. Requiring individual source/page review adds a dependency without replacing those final judgments; forbidding voluntary review serves no preservation property. Sampled checks cannot replace complete mechanical content coverage. Treating mechanical retention as final acceptance would leave appearance and truthful self-description unjudged.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:batch-conversion-review-consequences`

The exception is limited to eligible content conversion; every other change keeps its existing requirements, including recorded evidence, source-byte renewal and splitting work too large to review honestly. Protected scopes are ineligible; their binding and acceptance amendments belong to a separate protected-transfer design. No new acceptance field is authored by a conversion. Machinery changes name descriptions whose truth may be affected. The nineteen native acceptances retain mechanical checking under their separately authorized timing. Page/screen acceptance remains external; eligible main-line publication does not await that final acceptance. Final evidence includes author/reviewer designations, model-family independence and complete coverage. Retained publication evidence holds that record independently of authorship. Development reference tags do not claim final acceptance; the v0.6.0-dev.2 reference supplies both word and appearance baselines, and the release tag marks the reviewed state.

Scope admissibility alone leaves the retention gate incomplete: assignments must bind target identities, a source-form inventory, local layout fields, protected passage identities and reserved unit names; new unit data needs independent identity and assertion checks; checking inputs and effects and structural meaning need comparison with the parent; each gate attempt needs retained refusals and dispositions; and the capability revision must bind to a reviewed retained state. A fabricated generated artifact and a missing complementary check must each be refused by the complete gate before this exception applies.

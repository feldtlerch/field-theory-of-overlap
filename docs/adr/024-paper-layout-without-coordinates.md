# ADR 024: Paper layout without source coordinates · `rec:adr:024-paper-layout-without-coordinates`

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:paper:layout-without-coordinates`

No layout selection depends on a source line number. Local instructions attach to their paragraphs, displays, headings or box endings; they are covered by page review, and no layout-pin record is required. Instructions inside bound self-description passages change their exact source bytes and require renewed acceptance. A change to the theory, including a claim, a definition, the meaning of a formula or what the paper asserts, requires approval by the project's owner; every other change, including a meaning-preserving passage renewal, is accepted through independent review by a reviewer other than the change's author, with its evidence recorded. The author of a change never accepts it. When a claim of the theory is qualified or changed, the change is given to the project's owner for review before it lands. Page review judges the resulting appearance against the original look.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:paper:layout-without-coordinates-reasons`

Local attachment moves with its subject and removes the addressed-file hash dependency. The appendix symbol paragraph and the explainer checks formal note retain their line-breaking settings through local instructions. Exact source-byte acceptance identifies the resulting passages without weakening their boundary. Approval by the project's owner protects what the theory says, while review independent of the change's author protects every other change.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:paper:layout-without-coordinates-declined`

Retaining dead pin checks would preserve a coordinate obligation with no coordinate consumer. Replacing the pins with another address register would recreate the same dependency. Dropping the two line-breaking settings would change appearance to remove an address. Ignoring the inserted bytes would weaken exact passage acceptance. Requiring the project's owner to accept every renewed passage would spend the owner's attention on changes that alter no meaning and protect nothing that independent review does not.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:paper:layout-without-coordinates-consequences`

This supersedes ADR 023's clauses “The appendix symbol paragraph and the explainer checks formal note retain two line-addressed repairs while their accepted passage bytes remain unchanged; every retained line address has a matching whole-file pin.” and “Moving either instruction into its accepted passage requires separate renewal of that passage's acceptance.” It also supersedes “Source-local instructions select relocated paragraphs; only the two retained accepted-passage sites are selected by source coordinates.”, “Page repairs are site-local except for the two explicitly retained line-addressed sites; every retained coordinate has a matching pin.” and “The existing pin checks, identity and bibliography requirements, and the original look as the page-review baseline continue to apply.” Both repairs are local, and their resulting passage bytes require acceptance; pin checks have no remaining layout address to check, while identity and bibliography requirements and the original look as the page-review baseline continue to apply. The prior paragraph settings are restored, and display-entry assignments remain local to their display group.

This supersedes ADR 014's clause “Every line-addressed hook has a pin in `documents/paper-build/layout-pins.toml` naming its target file, lines and the file's hash; preparation refuses a hook address without a pin, a pin without a hook address, and a changed target, naming the hook before typesetting.” No layout-pin record or layout-pin preparation check remains. ADR 014's clause “These repairs change neither the document-wide measure and spacing nor the accepted self-description passages” is qualified: the repairs preserve the document-wide measure and spacing; local instructions inside self-description passages require acceptance of their resulting source bytes.

ADR 014's preparation clause “checks the closed source list, the layout pins and the pinned package and font bytes” becomes “checks the closed source list and the pinned package and font bytes”. ADR 016's release clause “source-list, layout-pin, support-pin, accepted-passage and PDF/receipt byte checks” becomes “source-list, support-pin, accepted-passage and PDF/receipt byte checks”. Its preparation clause “declared generated parts, source removal, pins and unchanged accepted bytes together” becomes “declared generated parts, source removal and accepted passage bytes together”. The nineteen exact passage checks, printed-identity check and PDF/receipt byte gate stay live. Page acceptance and publication permission remain separate from their mechanical checks.

This supersedes ADR 016's clause “every bound self-description passage is to carry a named renewal acceptor”. ADR 017's clause “changed bound passage bytes still require their named renewal acceptance” means renewed acceptance under this record. A meaning-preserving renewal records independent review and its evidence; a renewal changing the theory requires approval by the project's owner. ADR 017's requirement for independent page and screen review is unaffected.

# ADR 056: Checked result containers in corpus composition · `rec:adr:056-result-containers`

**Status:** Accepted.

**Decision (Statement)** · `dec:documents:checked-result-containers`

A registered insight, remark or definition with independently admitted plain paragraph children may be one result-form corpus slot. This form is distinct from the heading-form slot of ADR 055: it owns one keyed result header, its actual optional grade, any admitted local grade gap, and ordered children within one checked source interval. It retains the corpus-slot role and one-marker substitution, while the original heading-form admission remains as stated in ADR 055. A child kind is admitted only by its own checked interface; enclosing it in a result admits nothing by containment.

The header key is a paper mint with a distinct coarse-unit identity. The coarse unit declares one documentary target and places its selector once in its checked result header; the joint mint inventory records that placement as a unit paper mint and excludes it from strict-unit scope. Its title comes from the checked canonical paper heading binding and its number from the shared section/result counter event order, including unconverted preceding results. The plan compares that number, title, key, reference kind and destination with the typesetter's independent output. An absent grade remains absent. Insight's reference kind remains remark.

The paper emits the checked environment, header, grade, layout and ordered children through the existing theorem and audit interfaces. Markdown and HTML emit and recover the same checked header and child order with their own admitted result presentation. The compared native support selects the theorem and header closure from macros_overlap.tex, the pinned reference-type compatibility hook from compat.tex and the grade-gap command from reader-hooks.tex; the whole selected source files are declared inputs and compared bytewise, while unrelated source declarations and hooks are not copied into the module. It loads the necessary packages before that closure. Changed source, unsupported header or child, counter operation, binding, reference kind, event order, strict production setting, emitted content or recovered screen form refuses.

The complete-document comparison of ADR 043 and M077 retains its complete streams and unchanged-residual proof. A selected result has independent source correspondence for its header tokens, events and every admitted child; a result cannot hide content that the comparison cannot classify. The ordinary release and protected-passage checks remain.

**Reasons (Why)** · `arg:documents:checked-result-containers-reasons`

The existing importer already locates result boundaries, registered headers, grades, plain children and their complete byte partition. Reusing its draft and the existing corpus slot keeps one source walker and one ordered inclusion owner. The shared counter reader and canonical paper lookup avoid a second authored number or title.

**Declined (Alternatives)** · `arg:documents:checked-result-containers-declined`

Raw source replay would not be native composition. Treating the whole environment as a paragraph would lose header and child boundaries. Reading title or number from the auxiliary file would use the output as its own authority. Admitting mathematics or cells because they sit inside a result would bypass their independent checks.

**Consequences (Accepted)** · `arg:documents:checked-result-containers-consequences`

The result-form slot requires a checked header mint placement, shared number and title derivation, compared native support, three renderers, output recovery and a complete pair comparison for each selected occurrence. ADR 055's heading-form slot remains bounded to its existing kinds. Records 043 and 053 continue to require complete source and output correspondence. No result with an unresolved child is transferred.

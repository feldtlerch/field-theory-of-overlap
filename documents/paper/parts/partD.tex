\subsection{The report carries content and slant}
\label{subsec:rep:content}
\secgrade{nec}%
Accountability is not a field but a four-role structure --- actor $i$ who causes
$\outcomechange{k}$, subject $k$, witness $w$ who logs, reader $r$ who judges --- and only
$\outcomechange{k}$ is a field-fact, never directly observed. The witness logs it as they frame it.

\begin{definition}[\hdr{pr:report}]\grade{nec}
  With \ftag{g:split} separating the $\dimwelfare$ content from the $\dimwelfare$ distortion, the
  log carries content plus slant. The act itself is a goods \emph{vector} and a slant is a
  scalar; the two add only after the act is read on a published axis
  (\ftag{art:directive}, \ftag*{c:q}) --- one scalar coin for fact and slant alike:
  \PaperAlignSep{9pt}\begin{align}
    &\witnessreport{w} = \mathbf q\transpose\outcomechange{k} + \witnesscolour{w}
      \label{eq:pr:report}\\
    &\witnesscolour{w} = (\lean{w}{i}-\lean{w}{k})\,\witnessstake{w}
      &:& \eqtag{pr:colour}{$\dimratio\cdot\dimwelfare=\dimwelfare$, direction $\times$ magnitude}
  \end{align}
\end{definition}

A direction --- the difference of leans --- scaling a magnitude --- the witness's stake in
the act. It is the operator's two readings re-fused, the cousin of \ftag{nrm:incl}: the graph
splits $\comovfield$ into lean and coupling at the source and rejoins them here, once for inclusion,
once for colour. Pro-actor colour exculpates, pro-subject colour condemns, and equi-interest
($\lean{w}{i}=\lean{w}{k}$) gives colour $0$, faithful. The reader reconstructs the
de-coloured read,
$\reconstruct{k}{r}=\witnessreport{w}-\colourestimate{w}{r}$, and applies their own stake.

\subsection{Colour-span: when enemies agree}
\label{subsec:colour-span}
\secgrade{nec}%
One witness can be faithful by being equi-interested; a crowd can be faithful by spanning
colours. The first is \ftag{pr:woven}; the second is the ensemble mechanism already latent in
\ftag{pr:report}. A crowd is not yet an anchor: it must be independent twice, not copied and
not one colour.

\begin{definition}[\hdr{pr:gain}]\grade{nec}
  For an act by actor $i$ affecting subject $k$, witness $w$ has colour gain
  \begin{equation}
    \colourgain{w}:=\lean{w}{i}-\lean{w}{k}\qquad \dimratio.
    \label{eq:pr:gain}
  \end{equation}
  The gain is a difference of leans. Undefined leans are not zeros: a witness with no
  readable lean on one of the relevant edges is excluded from gain-based de-colouring until
  that edge is anchored, or else enters through a separate detached route. Felt-zero is not
  balance; ambivalence supplies a zero gain only when both leans are defined and equal.
\end{definition}

As algebra, \ftag{pr:gain} is \gradeword{nec}: once the two leans are supplied, their
difference is typed. Public use of $\colourgain{w}$ inherits \gradeword{cont-anch}, because
the leans are read through $\anchormetric$ and must be posted independently of the focal
report. If the report authors the gains by which it is de-coloured, the span is fabricated,
not measured.

\begin{definition}[\hdr{pr:span}]\grade{nec}
  For a finite witness set with defined gains, let
  \begin{equation}
    \meancolourgain=\frac1{\countof}\sum_w\colourgain{w},\qquad
    \meansqcolourgain=\frac1{\countof}\sum_w\colourgain{w}^{2},\qquad
    \varcolourgain=\meansqcolourgain-\meancolourgain^{2}.
    \label{eq:pr:span-moments}
  \end{equation}
  The colour-span, including the zero-colour corner, is
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{
    \colourspanof{\colourgainfamily}:=
    \begin{cases}
      1, & \meansqcolourgain=0,\\[4pt]
      \dfrac{\varcolourgain}{\meansqcolourgain}, & \meansqcolourgain>0.
    \end{cases}}
    \qquad \dimratio.
    \label{eq:pr:span}
  \end{equation}
  Hence $0\le\colourspan\le1$.
\end{definition}

The two $\colourspan=1$ corners are distinct. If every witness has
$\colourgain{w}=0$, there is no colour to remove: the fact is faithful without span. If the
gains oppose and balance, the fact is faithful by cancellation. If every witness carries the
same nonzero gain, the fact and slant are confounded: one colour, no bracket. Thus
$\colourspan$ names recoverability, not mere diversity.

\begin{proposition}[\hdr{pr:span-ident}]\grade{feas}
  In the local equal-noise, equal-colour-amplitude corner, with gains supplied independently
  of the focal report, read the report through its anchored scalar fact
  $\recoveryfact=\normG{\outcomechange{k}}$ as in the recovery appendix. Then
  \begin{equation}
    \witnessreport{w}
      =\recoveryfact+\colourgain{w}\colouramplitude+\spannoise{w},
    \qquad
    \recoveryfact,\colouramplitude,\spannoise{w}:\dimwelfare,\quad
    \colourgain{w}:\dimratio,\quad
    \spannoisevariance:\dimpublicwelfaresq,
    \label{eq:pr:span-model}
  \end{equation}
  and $\operatorname{Var}(\spannoise{w})=\spannoisevariance$. After profiling out the
  colour-amplitude, the information about the anchored scalar fact is
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \precisionof{\recoveryfact}
      =\frac{\countof\,\colourspan}{\spannoisevariance}.\ }
    \label{eq:pr:span-ident}
  \end{equation}
  Count supplies mass; colour-span supplies de-colourability. The count factor is the
  explicit count-grade already present in the summation: $\dimcount=\countgrade$ lowers to
  an intensive contribution only after pooling, as in \ftag{gen:ext}. A crowd of one colour
  carries no information about $\recoveryfact$ once its slant is unknown; two opposed colours
  carry the gap between them.
\end{proposition}

The local proof is the regression corner. If $\meansqcolourgain>0$, this is the intercept
precision in the regression of $\witnessreport{w}$ on $\colourgain{w}$:
\begin{equation}
  \spanregressordesign\transpose\spanregressordesign
  =
  \countof
  \begin{pmatrix}
    1 & \meancolourgain\\
    \meancolourgain & \meansqcolourgain
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Var}(\widehat{\recoveryfact})
  =
  \spannoisevariance\,
  \frac{\meansqcolourgain}{\countof\,\varcolourgain}.
  \label{eq:pr:span-proof}
\end{equation}

If $\meansqcolourgain=0$, the colour column vanishes and
$\witnessreport{w}=\recoveryfact+\spannoise{w}$, so
$\precisionof{\recoveryfact}=\countof/\spannoisevariance$. The two cases are exactly
\ftag{pr:span}. The matrix dimension of $\precisionof{\recoveryfact}$ is
$\dimpublicwelfareinvsq$ once the count-grade is read through the pooling convention.

\begin{corollary}[\hdr{pr:deltaspan}]\grade{feas}
  When authorship-independence and colour-span are orthogonal confounds, the public
  de-colouring density factors:
  \PaperDisplayParagraph{equation}{stretch=2em}\begin{equation}
    \spandensity:=\effdensity\,\colourspan\qquad \dimratio.
    \label{eq:pr:deltaspan}
  \end{equation}
  $\effdensity$ kills copies; $\colourspan$ kills cliques. They multiply only under the
  stated orthogonality. Without that condition the complete object is the joint
  author-colour distribution, not their product.
\end{corollary}

The complete object is the joint author-colour distribution; $\colourspan$ is only its
second-moment leading invariant. A polarized two-camp ensemble and a broad colour-spectrum
can share $\colourspan$ while differing in capture-resistance.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Obviousness.}
Verification has a cost $\verificationcost:\dimwelfare$. Cheap verification does not itself
create witnesses --- \zcref{W:lookout} remains a will --- but it lowers the cost of recruiting
them. As $\verificationcost\to0$, an obvious public fact can recruit witnesses across the
population's native colour distribution; if that distribution has nonzero span,
$\countanch\colourspan$ rises without authority. Proof-of-work is the engineered corner:
costly to produce, cheap to verify, so every colour can adjudicate it. Reproducibility is
the scientific corner: independent labs spend the cost to re-query the world. In all three,
the anchor is not a person but cross-colour convergence on a state.

\begin{econ}[label=e:pow, title={Costly proof splits credibility},
  ref=pr:span, cites={a:signaling}, departs=flatten,
  stopsat={one scalar credibility where the field splits burned stake, colour-span, and alignment}]
  \textcite{a:signaling}'s signal separates types when the false type pays more to mimic it.
  Proof-of-work is the same single-crossing shape in engineered form: costly to produce,
  cheap to verify. The field grants the mechanism and splits what it certifies. Costly proof
  anchors a spent magnitude, visible to every colour; cheap verification recruits
  colour-span; neither anchors alignment. Economics reads one scalar, credibility. The field
  reads expenditure, cross-colour convergence on a state, and the lean that remains
  uncertified. The departure is therefore one-axis flattening where the field needs the
  spent length, the bracketing span, and the alignment kept apart.
\end{econ}

Science is the clean institutional corner: independent replication is colour-span with the
world as anchor. A reproducible result is one on which opposed prior commitments converge
after re-querying the same state. Reproducibility anchors the fact, not the worth of the
research; the is/ought wall is the same type-check in institutional form.

A fact is what survives the colour sweep. A verdict is the colour sweep. Thus cross-colour
agreement can make the world agree on what happened, and still cannot make the world agree
what the actor is.

\subsection{Four factors on four edges}
\label{subsec:rep:factors}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{definition}[\hdr{bf:factors}]\grade{nec}
  The witness's effect on a reader is governed by four ratios, each on a distinct directed
  edge, which the single scalar ``reliability'' had conflated \ftag{g:split}:
  \PaperDisplayParagraph{align}{stretch=2em,wholewords}\begin{align}
    \text{independence} &= 1-|\lean{w}{i}|
      &:& \eqtag{bf:ind}{not the actor's puppet; anti-Sybil}\\
    \text{unbias} &= 1-\tfrac12|\lean{w}{i}-\lean{w}{k}|
      &:& \eqtag{bf:unb}{the balance, not the distance}\\
    \text{credibility} &= (\lean{r}{w})_{+}
      &:& \eqtag{bf:cred}{closeness $=$ de-colouring ability}\\
    \text{motivation} &= \tfrac{1-\lean{i}{w}}{2}\cdot\tfrac{1+\lean{w}{k}}{2}
      &:& \eqtag{bf:mot}{self-protective $\times$ sympathy}
  \end{align}
\end{definition}

\subsection{The silencing, derived}
\label{subsec:rep:silencing}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{insight}[\hdr{pr:silence}]\grade{nec}
  Credibility does double duty: $\lean{r}{w}$ is trust \emph{and} the capacity to subtract a
  known slant, so a biased witness is fully informative to their own circle (who de-colour)
  and corrupting only to those who cannot. This is structural and anchor-relative, not
  universal: the most-motivated witness --- the actor's enemy, by \ftag{bf:mot} --- is the
  least de-colourable to the actor's side. The victim's true report is discounted exactly
  where it most needs hearing, not by anyone's malice but by the geometry of the four edges.
\end{insight}

\begin{definition}[\hdr{pr:info}]\grade{nec}

  &\text{unbias}      &:& \ \bigl(1-\tfrac12|\lean{w}{i}-\lean{w}{k}|\bigr),\ \ftag{bf:unb}
    \label{eq:pr:info-unb}\\
  &\text{credibility} &:& \ (\lean{r}{w})_{+},\ \ftag{bf:cred}
    \label{eq:pr:info-cred}
  \end{align}
  This is the gate-stride \ftag{g:gate} at \zcref{tbl:sc:info}: information is zero if the witness
  is slanted (unbias $0$) \emph{or} unreachable (credibility $0$).
\end{definition}

\subsection{The functional}
\label{subsec:wit:functional}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{definition}[\hdr{pr:woven}]\grade{nec}
  The woven node is \emph{defined}, not described, as the witness credible to all and slanted
  toward none --- the maximin of recovered information:
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ w^\star=\argmax_{w}\ \min_{r}\ \info_r(w).\ }
    \label{eq:pr:woven}
  \end{equation}
\end{definition}

``Close to all, enemy to none'' is the gloss of this $\argmax\min$; the functional is the
object; the functional \emph{is} the fair judge, and the register that meets it is the
concordance's, at \ftag{e:smith}. Off the reachable set the $\min$ is degenerate: one
reader with $(\lean{r}{w})_{+}=0$ zeroes a candidate, so in a heterogeneous network every
candidate can tie at zero and the $\argmax$ selects nothing. The functional therefore
ranks only over readers a candidate can reach; the unreached remainder is the anchor
debt, and the built instance that pays it --- the institution manufactured equi-interested
--- is \zcref{T:justice}. The woven node is an ideal the telos approaches
(\ftag{nrm:telos}), not a guaranteed occupant.

\subsection{Two stationary points on one manifold}
\label{subsec:wit:manifold}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{insight}[\hdr{pr:equi}]\grade{nec}
  Unbias $=1$ cuts the colour-zero manifold $\lean{w}{i}=\lean{w}{k}$; symmetrized over all
  parties, the equi-interest manifold $\alignall{w}\equiv c$, on which $\info_r=c$. Two
  colour-zero configurations sit on it, separated only by credibility, and \ftag{g:knock} keeps
  them distinct without collapsing the entailment. The detachment corner $c=0$
  ($\alignall{w}\equiv0$) is unbiased and worthless --- $\info\equiv0$, de-colourable
  by no one, the view from nowhere, faithful and inert. The equi-interest corner $c\to1$
  ($\alignall{w}\equiv1$) is unbiased and maximally credible --- the view from
  everywhere, colour-zero by balance of attachments, not their absence. The maximin selects
  the high-$c$ corner: impartiality is not disinterest but equi-interest, and it must be
  \emph{built} --- the will raising $\leanbare$ toward all --- not assumed.
\end{insight}

The configuration is a $\dimratio$ cause; it \emph{entails} the distinct $\dimratio$
properties \{credible, unbiased, fair-judge\} and the $\dimwelfare$ property \{wise, enlarged
self\} --- entailment across dimensions, never the identity ``credible $=$ wise.''

\subsection{Aggregation is the null projection}
\label{subsec:verd:null-projection}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{theorem}[\hdr{pr:agg}]\grade{nec}
  A global verdict is a portable scalar $\meanverdict{i}$ with
  $\verdictstack{i}=\meanverdict{i}\,\mathbf 1+\orthverdict{i}$. Decompose along the all-readers direction with
  \ftag{g:proj}:
  \begin{equation}
    \verdictstack{i}=\underbrace{\meanverdict{i}\,\mathbf 1}_{\projone\verdictstack{i}}
      +\underbrace{\orthverdict{i}}_{\text{reader-indexed}}.
    \label{eq:verd:decompose}
  \end{equation}
  The reader-independent component $\meanverdict{i}$ is built on the externality de-coloured
  \emph{without} any reader's local $\lean{r}{w}$ --- exactly the witness-independence
  \ftag{J:anchor} proved non-computable and Sybil-fabricable. Therefore
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \text{absent an external anchor:}\quad
      \projone\,\verdictstack{i}\in\ker(\text{signal}),\qquad
      \text{all signal lives in }\orthverdict{i}.\ }
    \label{eq:pr:agg}
  \end{equation}
  In the recovery model the condition is the covering
  $\mathbf 1\in\operatorname{col}(Z_{\mathrm{int}})$ --- internally pinned frames spanning
  the constant direction --- under which the global verdict is non-identified.
\end{theorem}

Aggregation is the projection onto the \emph{kernel} of the signal: the global verdict is not
forbidden but the component proven to carry zero information; the antecedent that meets
this wall is the concordance's, at \ftag{e:hayek}.

\subsection{The certificate is null}
\label{subsec:verd:certificate}

\begin{theorem}[\hdr{pr:detach}]\grade{nec}
  A fact and a verdict lose colour differently. A fact-report has an intercept:
  \begin{equation}
    \colourreport{\colourcoordinate}
      =\recoveryfact+\colourcoordinate\,\colouramplitude.
    \label{eq:pr:fact-intercept}
  \end{equation}
  Removing colour leaves $\recoveryfact$. A per-reader verdict is
  \begin{equation}
    \readerverdict{r}{i}
      =
      -\reconstruct{k}{r}\,\lean{r}{k}.
    \label{eq:pr:verdict-colour}
  \end{equation}
  At the reader-detached corner,
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \lean{r}{k}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \readerverdict{r}{i}=0.\ }
    \label{eq:pr:colour-detached}
  \end{equation}
  A fact has a colour-invariant because it is a state with a world behind it; a verdict is
  proportional to the reader's stake and has no invariant except the detached zero.
\end{theorem}

This is not \ftag{pr:agg} repeated. \ftag{pr:agg} is epistemic: the global scalar is
non-identified because reader-independent de-colouring requires the anchor. \ftag{pr:detach}
is ontic: stripping reader-colour from a verdict strips the verdict itself. The two nulls
agree in consequence, not in mechanism.

Above the fabrication line a Sybil mints marks of either sign for free, so the count carries
no information. There is no throne to refuse: certificate and blacklist are category errors,
not prohibited acts. The flywheel is unharmed --- \emph{seen} enlargement still pays through
the record and the reader's own stake --- only the \emph{sum into a person} is empty.

\section{Convergence and forgeability}

\subsection{The telos}
\label{subsec:conv:telos}
\secgrade{nec}%
Tying your fate to the subject before you act makes the row follow the column.

\begin{theorem}[\hdr{nrm:telos}]\grade{nec}
  Let $\ownwelfarechange{k}:=\ownwelfarechangedef{k}$. If
  \begin{equation}
    \holding{i}{k}>0
    \quad\text{and}\quad
    \currency{i}{k}>0
    \qquad\forall k,
    \label{eq:conv:positive-holding}
  \end{equation}
  then every componentwise nonnegative perturbation with some positive component raises the
  extended self:
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\
      \ownwelfarechange{k}\ge0,
      \quad \exists k:\ownwelfarechange{k}>0
      \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
      \Delta\extself{i}
      =\sum_k\holding{i}{k}\currency{i}{k}\ownwelfarechange{k}>0.\ }
    \label{eq:conv:telos}
  \end{equation}
  Therefore, on the positive orthant, good and wise coincide.
\end{theorem}

The telos is a positivity lemma --- one line, nearly definitional --- and its
non-trivial content is the converse geometry:

\begin{theorem}[\hdr{nrm:wedge}]\grade{nec}
  On one edge with booking $\holding{i}{k}\currency{i}{k}$, in the plane of lived
  change against the other's gain, the good-not-wise region --- the martyr wedge ---
  has angular measure
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \theta_{\mathrm{wedge}}
      =\arctan\!\bigl(1/(\holding{i}{k}\currency{i}{k})\bigr),\ }
    \label{eq:nrm:wedge}
  \end{equation}
  and it never closes at finite holding: goodness strictly exceeds wisdom for every

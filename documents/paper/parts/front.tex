\begin{abstract}
\noindent A relationship is a directed edge carrying an operator $\comov{i}{j}$, the
co-movement of two nodes' graded outcomes. It is read four ways. Contracted through both
nodes' weightings it yields a standing alignment $\lean{i}{j}\in[-1,1]$, the cosine of a
caring covector against a feeling covector --- direction; contracted through an event it
yields an event alignment, the lean of that act. Its norm yields a capacity, and an act
moving through it a realized stake --- the magnitude of co-moving fate. Beneath all four,
prior to any shared anchor, lies the felt total: what the event costs its holder, by that
holder's caring.
Direction, capacity, realized stake, and felt total are not one quantity, and nearly every
confusion below is their conflation. We treat $\comovfield$ as a field over a network, read
through these quantities, locate its fixed points, and isolate the single input that moves
an edge between them: a rate $\will$ opposing the reward gradient --- a will in humans, a
behaviour in animals, an allocation in plants. Two dimensions run the apparatus ---
public welfare scalars are $\dimwelfare$, the will is a rate $\dimrate$ --- and one
epistemic posit organizes the rest: a converter is readable only where it is spent. Its
shadow under the poorest instrument is the familiar fact --- a state is readable at a
point; a rate is not. From this follow a
scope boundary that is reachability, a still center whose maintenance trembles visibly
while its private wage remains unreadable, a moral grid split across time, \emph{good} the
present predicate and \emph{wise} the forward; accountability a per-reader structure on
one coloured fact, summing to no verdict --- quantitatively within the linear reading,
qualitatively on the independence of anchors; and a life that spans every dimension at once
and carries no dimensionally-natural magnitude --- a place, not a height. The buildable
artifact is a signed externality log: gate on capacity, price realized stake, settle in
arrears, certify no one. What it must never price is alignment, the mere lean two fates
share. The moral center is the enlargement of a common that always contains the self, the
self extended along the edges actually held.
\end{abstract}

\section{The object, the stance, the generators}
\label{sec:generators}

\subsection{The object}
\label{subsec:gen:object}
\secgrade{nec}%
A node is any locus whose fate can co-move with another's. A node's outcome is graded,
$\outcomevector{i}\in\outcomespace=\bigoplus_a\dimgood{a}$ --- a plurality of
incommensurable goods, a single length only after a weighting collapses it. The edge $ij$
carries the co-movement operator,
\begin{equation}
  \comov{i}{j}:\outcomespace\to\outcomespace,\qquad
  \comovcomp{i}{j}{a}{b}:\dimgoodratio{a}{b},
  \label{gen:obj}
\end{equation}
how $j$'s goods move $i$'s. It is read scalar-ly here through its alignment
$\ftag{op:cos}$ $\lean{i}{j}\in[-1,1]$ and a stake beneath it; the construction is held at
$\ftag*{op:cos}$. On the alignment alone: $\leanbare>0$, $j$'s loss is to that degree $i$'s
loss; $\leanbare=0$, ambivalence --- a tie whose components cancel; the apparent stranger is
off the scale entirely ($\caringcovector{i}{j}=0$, no lean to read, yet $\comovfield\neq0$);
the genuine stranger has no fate to share ($\comovfield=0$); $\leanbare<0$, shared
enmity, $j$'s loss is $i$'s gain. The object of study is $\comovfield$ as a \emph{field over a network} --- its
equilibria, their stability, and the one input that moves an edge between them.

\begin{definition}[\hdr{gen:goods}]\grade{nec}
  Readable edges live in the shared goods-space, the co-movement operator ranges over
  shared goods, and a node's welfare scalar is node-graded:
  \begin{align}
    \outcomespace &= \bigoplus_a \dimgood{a}
      \label{eq:gen:goods}\\
    \comovcomp{i}{j}{a}{b} &: \dimgoodratio{a}{b}
      \label{eq:gen:Srange}\\
    \selfweight{i} &: \dimnodepergood{i}{a}, \qquad
      \privatewelfare{i} : \dimnodewelfare{i}
      \label{eq:gen:wgrade}
  \end{align}
  Goods outside a shared representation are outside that edge's read, not denied.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\hdr{gen:grade}]\grade{nec}
  A grading is an index on a dimension whose sectors do not add without a posted converter.
  A grading is type-checked like a dimension but carries no independent weight in a norm.
  The apparatus has two fundamental dimensions,
  \begin{equation}
    \dimspace = \{\dimwelfare,\ \dimrate\},
    \label{eq:gen:dimspace}
  \end{equation}
  with $\dimratio$ their ratio-space.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\hdr{gen:ext}]\grade{nec}
  A pool lowers degree:
  \begin{align}
    \dimcount &:= \countgrade, \qquad \dimratio := \intensivegrade,
      \label{eq:gen:extdef}\\
    [\cdot]\sb{1}/\dimcount &= [\cdot]\sb{0}.
      \label{eq:gen:pool}
  \end{align}
  Thus $N\dimcount$ while $N/\countpool$ is intensive.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[\hdr{gen:trich}]\grade{nec}
  \begin{equation}
    \begin{array}{c|c|c}
      \text{plurality} & \text{converter} & \text{structure} \\
      \hline
      \text{goods} & \anchormetric & \text{flat public metric} \\
      \text{persons} & \currency{i}{j} & \text{curved interpersonal currency} \\
      \text{traits} & \text{none} & \text{non-normable profile}
    \end{array}
    \label{eq:gen:trich}
  \end{equation}
\end{definition}

The alphabet is two fundamental dimensions plus ratio-space and count-grade,
\begin{equation}
  \dimspace=\{\,\dimwelfare,\ \dimrate\,\};\qquad
  \dimratio\text{ is ratio-space};\qquad
  \dimcount=\countgrade\text{ is count-grade},
  \label{gen:alph}
\end{equation}
No unenumerated grading is used by the apparatus. Shared goods are plural,
$\outcomespace=\bigoplus_a\dimgood{a}$: a length only under a weighting, privately by a
held $\selfweight{i}$ (dark, \ftag{snk:ker}) or publicly by an anchored metric
$\anchormetric$ (\ftag{op:cos}). No third option makes a graded space a length, and the held
collapse is kept, not given (\ftag{ten:self}). The one fact:
\PaperDisplayParagraph{equation}{stretch=2em}\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\textbf{a state is readable at a point; a rate is not.}}
  \label{eq:gen:fact}
\end{equation}
Outcomes are states; the will is a rate. The fact is stated for the poorest instrument
--- passive, noiseless, a single instant --- and it is that instrument's \emph{shadow}
of the epistemic posit stated next (\ftag{epi:shadow}): richer records read more, and
the posit says exactly how much more.

\subsection{The two posits}
\label{subsec:gen:posits}
\secgrade{nec}%
The apparatus rests on two typed posits, one per basin, and neither is proven here. Each
is stated where only the construction needs it, corroborated downstream, settled in
arrears; the back-edges that carry the warrants are posted, every one, in the evidential
register (\zcref{tbl:ev}). The symmetry is the design.

\begin{postulate}[\hdr{gen:will}]\grade{nec}\PaperParagraph{stretch=2em}
  The ontic posit --- four claims under one name, each with its own modality, stated
  where only the construction needs them. \emph{Existence} \handle{gen:w-exists}
  (\gradeword{cont}): there is
  an input to edge dynamics beyond reward and decay. \emph{Type} \handle{gen:w-rate}
  (\gradeword{feas}):
  the input is a rate $\will\ \dimrate$ --- no reservoir behind it. \emph{Direction}
  \handle{gen:w-opp}
  (\gradeword{cont}): the connective will opposes the reward gradient
  $\nabla\rewardfield$ --- appetitive, gradient-opposing, a population claim.
  \emph{Exogeneity} (\gradeword{nec}): its source carries no dimension and nothing in
  the field grips it --- the ground itself (\ftag{src:nd}), the one component that is
  and stays axiomatic. No warrant is reachable here, all of it lying downstream of
  crossings forbidden by the basin split (\ftag{gen:basins}); it is collected at the
  data layer --- the measured decay (\ftag{ev:decay-occ}), the release knee
  (\ftag{ev:knee-rate}), the identified residual (\ftag{ev:resid-ex},
  \ftag{ev:resid-opp}) --- under posted
  identification conditions (\ftag{maint:ident}), transmitted by \ftag{J:occ},
  settled by \ftag{snk:anch-t}.
\end{postulate}

\begin{postulate}[\hdr{epi:conv}]\grade{nec}
  The epistemic posit: \textbf{a converter is readable only where it is spent.} A
  weighting, a currency, a metric --- any exchange-rate between incommensurables --- has
  observable consequences exactly at its transactions, and none at rest. Warrant
  downstream at \ftag{op:afriat} (\ftag{ev:afriat-conv}: rationalization recovers every
  spent gradient and nothing unspent), at \ftag{J:occ} (the maintainer's price), and at
  every stall the ledger records (\ftag{P:leak}).
\end{postulate}

\begin{corollary}[\hdr{epi:shadow}]\grade{nec}
  For the passive, noiseless, single-instant instrument, \ftag{epi:conv} specializes to
  the fact \zcref{eq:gen:fact}: a state is readable at a point; a rate is not
  ($\dimrate$-at-rest spends nothing \emph{into that instrument}). Richer instruments
  read more --- persistence, trembling, relaxation --- and the principle says exactly how
  much more: everything spent into them, nothing that is not. The fact is scoped to
  that instrument and to no other: the time-series record reads persistence, the
  fluctuation record reads maintenance (\ftag{J:tremble}), the probe reads restoring
  force, and what no instrument reads is the converter spent into none of them
  (\ftag*{epi:conv}).
\end{corollary}

One may assume naively that the fact is the light and the principle its elaboration; the
dependence runs the other way --- the fact is the shadow the principle casts on the
poorest instrument, and the still center's population is scoped by exactly that
dependence: darkness is the set of held, unposted converters, with the unspendable
ground beneath (\ftag{src:nd}).

\subsection{A life is a place, not a height}
\label{subsec:gen:place}
\secgrade{nec}%
\handle*{P:sig}\handle*{P:dens}\handle*{P:will}\handle*{P:dir}%
\handle*{P:nar}\handle*{P:bld}\handle*{P:res}%
A node's traits form a point
\begin{equation}
  \profile=\bigl(\underbrace{\traitsigma}_{\dimratio},\ \underbrace{\traitdensity}_{\dimratio},\
  \underbrace{\traitwill}_{\dimrate},\ \underbrace{\traitdirection}_{\dimrate},\
  \underbrace{\traitnarration}_{\dimnodewelfare{i}},\ \underbrace{\traitbuilding}_{\dimrate},\
  \underbrace{\traitrestraint}_{\dimrate}\bigr),
  \label{P:vec}
\end{equation}
entanglement, witnessability, maintenance, direction, self-narration, institution-building,
and restraint. The space spans three incommensurable dimensions, and the outcome axis
$\dimnodewelfare{i}$ is itself private, so the incommensurability runs within an axis as
well as across. Any agency-score
$\normof{\profile}^2\sim\dimratio+\dimrate\sp{2}+\dimnodewelfare{i}\sp{2}$ adds unlike units.
It is not hard to compute; it is type-incorrect, like taking one Euclidean norm across
length and temperature coordinates. The claim is \ftag{P:nonorm}, paid where the coordinates are defined; here it stands
as the object. A life carries no dimensionally-natural magnitude --- \ftag{g:knock} on the
grandest sentence the document states.

\subsection{Two basins}
\label{subsec:gen:basins}
\secgrade{nec}\handle*{gen:basins}%
The generators fall into two independent sources. The epistemic basin
$\{\ftag*{gen:alph},\ftag*{epi:fact}\}$ asks what can be known --- readability, scope, the
kernel. The ontic basin $\{\ftag*{gen:obj},\ftag*{gen:will}\}$ asks what is and what moves ---
the field, its gradients, its forcing term. Upstream they run separately; they meet exactly
once, at the flow \ftag{dyn:flow}, the heart --- and their instruments fail at one shared
floor, where the ontic engine that constructs a will (\ftag{op:afriat}) and the epistemic
fact that the net never yields its components (\ftag{epist:duhem}) point into the same dark,
\ftag{src:nd}\ \ftag{snk:two}. The meeting acts; the floor goes silent. Everything before it is
one basin or the other; everything after is the consequence of their confluence. One
consequence is reflexive: the warrant for \ftag*{gen:will}'s empirical components ---
instruments of the epistemic basin corroborating ontic claims, an epistemic reading
fixing an ontic type --- is a pre-confluence
cross-basin inference, architecturally unavailable until the flow. The will cannot
be argued early; the deferral is forced, not lazy.

\subsection{Audit}
\label{subsec:gen:audit}
\secgrade{nec}%
Theorems are distinctions forced by a definition, a derivative-sign, or a dimension. Three
struts brace the construction: \handle{g:knock}, equate two
dimensions and a mismatch kills the identity; \handle{g:proj},
split a map into image and kernel;
\handle{g:rec}, recover a degenerate corner on a restricted
domain. The dimensional form:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\text{no equating state}=\text{rate; no sampling a rate at a point;
         no scalarizing a relation.}}
\end{equation}
Register discipline \handle{mod:reg}: silence marks the
structurally unavailable, never the forbidden; no result below is a prohibition unless an
agent declines an available act.

Three further gaits read the construction rather than guard it:
\handle{g:split}, part one object into two by a finer reading;
\handle{g:gate}, the gate-stride $X=g\cdot f$, an on/off
magnitude times an amplifier; and \handle{g:sign}, read a
result off the sign of a derivative. Six gaits in all, the document's whole repertoire of
moves.

\subsection{Covenant}
\label{subsec:gen:covenant}
\secgrade{nec}%
Two membranes stay loose while the body densifies. The scholar's, \registername{[econ]}, locates
each result in its economic antecedent and marks where it departs --- the document's one
flourish; it carries \ftag{epist:duhem}, the one citation the framework does not annex but
meets at the floor --- no departure, no Concordance-E row, because here the apparatus agrees
rather than departs. The human's, the eight virtue-words and the plain refrains, welds each
to a formal part and renders clean: a single carried point, never expanded. The visible
labels are the connective tissue --- a result stated once under its tag is referenced
forever by that tag, never by relative phrasing or section number. The scope boundary is
reachability: whether incentive, cost, or argument can grip the input --- and grip is a
susceptibility. The bind-without-pull register --- duty kept against inclination --- is
an \emph{open-loop} input: it carries a forcing term, as whatever holds an edge must;
it takes no field quantity as an argument; and it is out of scope as a \emph{target} ---
in scope as a term, ungrippable as a lever, neither modeled further nor judged
(\see{subsec:still:deontic}). This sets the
readability joint \handle{J:read}: the joint between the two posits, \ftag*{gen:will} and
\ftag*{epi:conv}, where what can move meets what can be read --- all that can be known at
all is known through what is spent, and the poorest instrument's shadow of that is the
state/rate split (\ftag*{epi:shadow}).

\begin{econ}[label=e:input, title={The borrowed move, one level up},
  ref=gen:will, cites={a:degustibus,a:mechdesign}, departs=annex,
  stopsat={annexes the readable; cannot host a moving input or reach the rate at rest}]
  The move is economics' own, run one level up, and the borrowed license must be named
  before it is spent. \textcite{a:degustibus} --- \emph{de gustibus non est disputandum}
  --- take preferences as exogenous and build a complete science downstream; mechanism
  design --- Hurwicz, Myerson, Maskin, Roth, Milgrom \parencite{a:mechdesign} --- designs
  rules so that, \emph{given} preferences, good outcomes follow, agnostic about where
  preferences come from. We place one undischarged input \ftag*{gen:will} and derive the rest:
  the \emph{move} is identical, and so is its warrant --- we impose a type no measurement
  supplies and run what it forces, exactly as economics imposes ``preference is a complete
  preorder'' and runs the welfare theorems. Granted the bridge, the machinery is valid; the
  bridge is granted or it is not. This is the whole epistemic contract, and it has a precise
  reach. Four type-posits are \emph{co-liable} with the discipline --- outcomes-as-states
  against preference-as-preorder, the revealed frame, the scalar aggregate, the two anchors:
  deny our typing of these and you have denied the move your own field is built on. One
  posit is ours alone and carries no economic cover, because economics works in a single
  currency and never needs it --- that the alphabet \ftag*{gen:alph} is \emph{closed}, no
  fifth fundamental dimension hiding in the social ontology. That is the seam to cut if you
  would break the method, and we name it rather than hide it. The departure from the
  borrowed move is type: their input is a parameter the machine reads at rest, $\dimwelfare$;
  ours is a force opposing the field the machine computes, $\dimrate$, against
  $\nabla\rewardfield$ --- and where mechanism design optimizes an objective over outcomes,
  the artifact it licenses optimizes nothing it records (\see{sec:artifact}, the objective
  deleted, the deletion forced not weakened). The agnosticism about the input's origin is
  the wall the discipline cannot cross --- and the wall, not the agnosticism, is what the
  rest of this document maps.
\end{econ}

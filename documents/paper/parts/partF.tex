\subsection{The recession-dual}
\label{subsec:decep:recession}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{definition}[\hdr{op:recess}]\grade{nec}

  \begin{equation}
    \falsereadout:\ \text{L-cell}\ \longmapsto\ \text{orthant-good},\qquad
    \falsereadout\neq\readout\ \text{only on the L}.
    \label{eq:op:recess}
  \end{equation}
\end{definition}

Its currency is the earned affect of genuine virtue \ftag{w:vir}, spoofed exactly, separable
only by $\operatorname{sign}(\alignrate)$ against the corrected baseline --- two points the
actor lacks at the instant \ftag{op:vel}.

\subsection{The L-restriction}
\label{subsec:decep:Lrestrict}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{proposition}[\hdr{op:L}]\grade{nec}
  The two maps disagree only where there is something to hide:
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \falsereadout=\readout\ \text{on the non-negative orthant; they part only
      on the L.}\ }
    \label{eq:decep:L}
  \end{equation}
\end{proposition}

By \ftag{g:rec}, genuine good in the orthant has nothing to falsify --- no concealment
pressure, only the imprecision of a dark rate. That concealment pressure arises only
where there is something to hide is a \emph{psychology} premise, and it is marked as
one: the formal content is the cell-classification --- where the maps may part ---
never a within-cell magnitude. Honest love is not deceived about its
\emph{cell}; it cannot certify its fine grain, but nothing is corrupting it. The active
falsification is confined to the L, and --- since magnitude was never a quantity
\ftag{P:nonorm} --- what stays uncertified in the bright orthant is not a lie but the absence
of a number that never existed. The bleak reading, that you never know if you are good, is
the over-extension; the L-restriction is the bound.

\subsection{The self-nondeception floor}
\label{subsec:decep:floor}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{definition}[\hdr{nrm:floor}]\grade{nec}
  The self-nondeception floor is the boundary separating acts on which the readout
  $\readout$ and the self-readout $\falsereadout$ agree from L-acts that the

The floor is a disagreement between two readouts and nothing more. It infers no motive,

locates is the cell where the two maps part --- the same cell-classification
\ftag{op:L} bounds, read from the actor's side.

\subsection{Three coins, and the funded fake}

This is underdetermination over a \emph{known} palette \ftag{epist:duhem} (\gradeword{nec}): the

The escapability is the danger. \ftag{src:nd} lies beneath this layer, not on it: not unfixed

\subsection{The second prediction}
\label{subsec:decep:second-prediction}
\secgrade{cont}%
Self-narration $\traitnarration$ is inducible, not fixed, and it is what self-deception rides on: to
narrate an L-act to oneself as enlargement requires the narrative scaffold
\ftag{op:recess} installs. The prediction: a great ape enculturated among humans acquires
$\traitnarration$ with language, and with it the self-deception shadow --- graded self-deceptive
misreport in proportion to the narrative scaffold, where wild conspecifics show restraint and
consolation without narration and \emph{no} promotion of $w$ to $\alignfact$. The
capacity for self-deception is then partly transmissible by language, because it rides on
narration.

The claim is softer than the spectral prohibition \ftag{dyn:rot}: its discriminator ---
whether the misreport is \emph{self}-believed --- is by \ftag{epi:fact} unreadable at the
instant, so the framework offers this as a research program with a built-in observability
limit and rests its falsifiable standing on the spectral claim.

\subsection{The deontic, typed}

\begin{definition}[\hdr{deon:open}]\grade{nec}
  Decompose any forcing control-theoretically: a feedback part, whose arguments are
  field quantities, and an open-loop part with no arguments at all. The
  \textbf{susceptibility} $\suscept$ is the derivative of the forcing with respect to
  the field quantities --- grip, made a number:
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \textbf{bind}:=\suscept\equiv0\ \text{(open-loop)};\qquad
      \textbf{pull}:=\suscept\neq0\ \text{(feedback)}.\ }

  \end{equation}

  forcing term} --- whatever holds an edge must (\zcref{eq:J:occ:exact}). It is

  The duality completes the two feet: $\ker\readout$ is the unobservable subspace;

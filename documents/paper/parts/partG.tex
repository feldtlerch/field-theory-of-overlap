\section{The signed externality log}
\label{sec:artifact}

\subsection{Directive}
\label{subsec:artifact:directive}
\secgrade{cont-anch}%
\handle*{nrm:anchmetric}\handle*{nrm:anchbase}%
The buildable artifact has one directive.
\begin{statement}{art:directive}{The directive}\grade{cont-anch}
  Record the signed externality, both valences, per act --- its \textbf{settlement}
  the signed read $\settlement{k}$ through a \textbf{published axis}
  $\logaxis{a}$ (\ftag*{c:q}, posted per entry, criticizable), its \textbf{gate} the
  capacity $\capgate{i}{k}$, its \textbf{alignment} (the lean) only logged, never the
  basis of the charge. A signed externality cannot be a norm; the log's own reading
  axis is therefore a covector, and it is published --- the log's lean is on the
  record, where every other lean is. Anchor belief in independence. Build no global
  verdict --- none crosses the anchor wall.
\end{statement}

An act touches three registers: the externality $\outcomechange{k}\ \dimwelfare$, the internality
$\Delta\extself{i}\ \dimnodewelfare{i}$ (private), and the will $\will\ \dimrate$ (a rate, dark). Only
the first is buildable --- and it is buildable because it touches \textbf{both feet}: anchored
in metric (\ftag*{nrm:anchmetric}, $\anchormetric$, present) and in counterfactual
(\ftag*{nrm:anchbase}, $\outcomechange{k}$, \ftag{snk:anch}, forward). ``Observer-independent''
holds for its metric, not its baseline; the externality spans the two settled-later anchors
and never the kernel. Charging on the lean would price \ftag{op:cos}, a $\dimratio$ a party
dissolves by re-weighting; charging on the settlement prices a posted covector read,
and the sign-blind magnitude beneath it is \ftag{f:stake}, the $\dimwelfare$ that
resists both drifts. Price what moves, never the way it leans.

\subsection{Four buildables}
\label{subsec:artifact:buildables}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{serieslist}
  \bitem{B:record}{Record} Global, signed, per-act, callable log of $\outcomechange{k}$, coarse ---
    ``harmed/helped a known party,'' never a score; refinement only sharpens a counterfeiter's
    target, symmetric in both signs. The entry is a coloured report \ftag{pr:report}, so \zcref{B:record}
    records what readers need to de-colour it: the witness identity, a $t_0$ baseline stamp on
    $\leanbare$ --- separating \emph{built the friendship} ($\leanbare:0\to+$, \ftag{w:enl}) from
    \emph{friends already} ($\leanbare:+\to+$, \ftag{ten:fid}), a $\Delta\leanbare$ unreadable from
    the endpoint --- and a non-beneficiary co-witness for the enlargement half. Settlement is
    factored: the harm half discounts in \ftag{f:path} (depth-$k$, hard-zero under concealment),
    the enlargement half in \ftag{f:local} (depth-one, clannish). One operator, opposite
    frictions.

  \bitem{B:belief}{Belief} $\effdensity$ (\ftag{f:eff}) for authorship-independence,
    and $\colourspan$ (\ftag{pr:span}) for colour-span where the claim must cross circles.
    $\effdensity$ kills copies; $\colourspan$ kills cliques. The public belief density is
    the pair $(\effdensity,\colourspan)$, with the product $\spandensity=\effdensity\colourspan$
    licensed only under orthogonality (\ftag{pr:deltaspan}); not internally bootstrappable
    \ftag{J:anchor}.

  \bitem{B:relevance}{Relevance} Care-weight scaling with the reader's own overlap
    $\lean{r}{k}$ ($\leanbare$-graded circles); relational, observer-local.

  \bitem{B:salience}{Salience} Sunk, unrecoverable cost that \emph{measures nothing} but
    lifts a candidate above the noise floor, rationing \zcref{W:lookout}'s scarce judgment --- sunk, not
    staked (recoverable stake recurses to plutocracy), applied at every dense scale, the
    witness-pool constant $\countpool$ made checkable. Salience is deliberate recruitment of
    span when obviousness will not recruit it for free. It is proof-of-work in the narrow
    sense: burned cost raises the chance that witnesses of more than one colour will look.
    The cost measures no virtue and certifies no verdict; it only buys a chance at
    colour-span.
\end{serieslist}

\subsection{Three refusals}
\label{subsec:artifact:refusals}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{serieslist}
  \ritem{R:nostake}{No stake} No $\leanbare$ between the log and any recorded outcome --- which
    is also what gives the log network-wide credibility: a log of colour $0$ on the system is
    de-colourable by all \ftag{bf:cred}. A staked log is a correlated witness,
    $\effdensity_{\text{log}}=0$ --- the surveillance machine.

  \ritem{R:trusted}{Watch the trusted most}\PaperParagraph{stretch=2em} Trust withdraws option-surveillance \ftag{op:read},
    a predator's runway; equal watching under-watches where arbitrage pays. Impartial in rule,
    unequal in outcome, keyed to trust.

  \ritem{R:askonly}{Ask, never compel} Compulsion manufactures compeller-correlated records
    ($\effdensity=0$) and stakes the log. Carve-out: compel cross-examinable \emph{facts about
    an act}, never a \emph{standing edge}.
\end{serieslist}

\subsection{Four theorems}
\label{subsec:artifact:theorems}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{serieslist}
  \titem{T:certnull}{The certificate is null} Derived as the null projection \ftag{pr:agg}:
    per-reader verdicts admit no aggregate, real in a shared anchor, null across; no throne.
    Under covering,
    \begin{equation}
      \precision_{\mathrm{certificate}}
      \le
      \mathbf 1\transpose\pinv{\frameloading\anchorprior\frameloading\transpose}\mathbf 1,
      \label{eq:T:certnull-ceiling}
    \end{equation}
    and this vanishes when all frames are internally pinned.

  \titem{T:audited}{Audited identity}
    \begin{equation}
      \boxed{\ \parbox{0.85\linewidth}{\centering
        the surveillance machine minus stake, minus compulsion, minus the global verdict ---
        and the global verdict was never real.}\ }
      \label{eq:T:audited}
    \end{equation}

  \titem{T:wound}{The wound} \zcref{B:salience} collides with \zcref{W:connect}: every cost-anchor that excludes the costless
    bot excludes the genuine-poor by the same coin. The self-inclusion floor (widen) and the
    Sybil anchor (charge entry) cannot both be maximized; the cut is always a wound. It has a
    dimensional face: \zcref{B:salience} rations by a cost that measures stake, and a held weighting can make a
    genuine stake read as neutral lean (\ftag{f:ambiv}), so the costless-bot cut and the
    ambivalent-poor cut fall by the same coin.

  \titem{T:justice}{Justice is built reach, and built credibility} The harm half zeros
    under concealment (depth-$k$); standing institutions hold \ftag{f:path} $>0$ for the severed
    --- and more, they \emph{manufacture the ideal witness}. Fair belief of the victim is
    structurally impossible (their motivation is their bias \ftag{bf:mot}), so justice supplies
    the equi-interested institution \ftag{pr:woven}: close to all parties, enemy to none, \zcref{R:nostake}
    giving the colour-$0$ disinterest and role-pay-duty giving the motivation the
    merely-disinterested lack.
    \begin{equation}
      \boxed{\ \parbox{0.86\linewidth}{\centering
        Justice $=$ standing bridges to the severed; the witness no victim can be. Good deeds
        self-witness (depth-one); harm requires built reach (depth-$k$).}\ }
      \label{eq:T:justice}
    \end{equation}
\end{serieslist}

\subsection{Four wills it rests on}
\label{subsec:artifact:wills}
\secgrade{nec}%
The log reads welfare states $\dimwelfare$, weights by ratios $\dimratio$, and runs on rates $\dimrate$
it cannot build.

\begin{serieslist}
  \witem{W:connect}{to connect} \ftag{w:con}: fills it; inert without.

  \witem{W:lookout}{to look from outside} Cuts the fake on \zcref{B:salience}'s thinned set; self-funding
    (densification is enlargement) but must be exercised.

  \witem{W:keepers}{keepers' restraint} \zcref{R:nostake} is the highest-rent capture, broker over every
    edge, and keeping it stake-free is an occupied-kernel act \ftag{ten:res} against an option
    only keepers fully know, terminating at no meta-ledger. This is the guard against the
    captured authority (\see{subsec:conv:forge}): the most dangerous forgery is a judge
    everyone loves, and \zcref{W:keepers} is the single restraint that refuses to become one --- the one
    genuine restraint, the sole silence in the document that is a \emph{refusal} and not an
    absence \ftag{mod:reg}.

  \witem{W:seed}{the seed} The per-act reward is noise below threshold density \ftag{dyn:bif};
    the frame cannot start itself. Colour-span lowers the density threshold by making public
    facts cheaper to anchor, but it does not supply the first will to look. \ftag{W:seed}
    remains a will; \ftag{pr:span} lowers its cost.
\end{serieslist}

\begin{theorem}[\hdr{art:keepers}]\grade{cont-anch}
  Split the keepers by susceptibility (\zcref{deon:open}): a fraction $q$ hold
  restraint at $\suscept=0$ --- unpriced, no reservation price at all --- and the rest
  hold priced restraint, a will with an elasticity. Write $R_{\mathrm{cap}}$ for the
  log's capture rent, which grows with its adoption. Every priced restraint is
  eventually out-bid by a rent that grows without bound, so integrity is monotone
  decreasing in the rent and
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \lim_{R_{\mathrm{cap}}\to\infty}\operatorname{integrity}(R_{\mathrm{cap}})=q.\ }
    \label{eq:art:keepers}
  \end{equation}
  At scale, the log's security converges to exactly the fraction of keepers whose
  restraint is unpriced --- behaviorally indistinguishable from the deontic, by the
  one-sidedness of \zcref{deon:test}. At any finite rent the keepers' restraint is
  \ftag{ten:res}: a will, priced, in scope. In the limit the load transfers
  continuously onto the unpriced remainder, so the register the covenant rules out of
  scope as a target holds the one parameter the artifact's asymptotic security equals
  --- load-bearing in proportion to the log's own success.
\end{theorem}

\refrain{}{The surveillance machine minus stake, minus compulsion, minus the global verdict ---
and the global verdict was never real.}

\begin{econ}[label=e:externality, title={The externality instruments, disarmed},
  ref=B:record, cites={a:pigou,a:coase}, departs=flatten,
  stopsat={Pigou prices the act, Coase settles toward a verdict; the log prices stake not lean and settles toward none}]
  The log is the discipline's own instruments, assembled and then disarmed. It is the
  Pigouvian externality-pricer \parencite{a:pigou} --- charge what an act does to a third party
  --- and the Coasean settlement \parencite{a:coase} --- the externality persists because
  transaction costs block the bargain, so settle it in arrears where the bargain failed ---
  \emph{agreeing} with both, on one point of departure each. Pigou prices the act; the log
  prices its \textbf{stake} and never its \textbf{lean} (the $\dimwelfare$ that resists
  re-weighting, never the $\dimratio$ a party dissolves at will). Coase's parties settle
  \emph{toward} a verdict; the log settles toward \emph{no} verdict (\zcref{T:certnull}: the global scalar is
  null, \ftag{e:repeated} the repeated-games reading of the same wall).
\end{econ}

\begin{econ}[label=e:artifact, title={Mechanism design with the objective deleted},
  ref=pr:agg, cites={a:tadelis}, departs=scalar,
  stopsat={the designer's objective removed because the global verdict it would optimize carries zero information; reputation and credit are that machine re-armed}]
  The log is mechanism design \parencite{a:mechdesign} with the objective deleted --- which is
  the precise sense in which \ftag{e:input} called the move ``identical'': the same placement of
  an exogenous input and the same downstream derivation, but the designer's objective function
  over outcomes \emph{removed}, because the thing it would optimize, the global verdict, is the
  component proven to carry zero information \ftag{pr:agg}. Optimizing nothing it records globally
  is not a weaker mechanism design; it is the only one consistent with the null projection. The
  surveillance machine is precisely this log with the deletions undone --- stake added, consent
  removed, the global verdict sold --- and reputation and credit systems \parencite{a:tadelis}
  are that machine, holding stake, running on unasked participation, selling the verdict \zcref{T:certnull}
  proves null.
\end{econ}

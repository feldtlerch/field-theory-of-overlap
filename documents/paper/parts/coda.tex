\subsection{The three tenses}
\label{subsec:coda:tenses}
\secgrade{nec}%
Every reachable thread in the document terminates at one of two attractors, and
\ftag{g:knock} holds them apart as the last distinction it draws --- both now named in
one coin:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\ \underbrace{\text{spent-privately}}_{\text{held, read by a biography
    (\ftag*{snk:ker})}}\ \Big/\
    \underbrace{\text{not-yet-spent}}_{\text{settled in arrears (\ftag*{snk:anch-t})}}\ \Big/\
    \underbrace{\text{unspendable}}_{\text{the ground (\ftag*{src:nd})}}\ }
  \label{eq:coda:feet}
\end{equation}
Three tenses of one verb. The first two are the feet \handle{snk:two}: \ftag{snk:ker} is
the still center, what an instrument reads only through the holder's own transactions;
\ftag{snk:anch} is the fabrication line and the forward predicate, what no instrument
settles now but time may settle later (both halves; the self-applied case at
\see{app:self-limit} carries only \ftag*{snk:anch-t}). There is no third foot. The third
tense is not a foot but the ground the feet stand on: a converter spent into no currency
is not slow to read; there is nothing to read, by type. The one thread that escapes both
feet --- the deontic --- escapes them not as a third terminus but onto that ground: it
runs to \ftag{src:nd}, and terminating there \emph{is what out-of-scope means}. The
record's instruments run a ladder --- persistence, trembling, release, circulation
(\ftag{epi:shadow}, \ftag{J:tremble}, \ftag{dyn:pitch}, \ftag{dyn:loop}) --- and each
rung removes something from the dark and \emph{nothing from this residue}. That is what
a well-founded darkness looks like: not a retreating shadow but a fixed complement, the
ledger's, exactly. And one darkness on this ground is \emph{asymmetric}: the bind label
is refutable, never verifiable (\zcref{deon:test}) --- neither foot nor ground but a
one-way membrane. Nor is it alone: the will's identification flags misfit and never
certifies honesty; the reservoir is bounded and never excluded; the funded fake settles
only in arrears; the broker is detected only against an anchored counterfactual. Every
deep warrant in this document is one-sided --- falsification-bright,
verification-dark --- and that is not an accident of method but \ftag{epi:conv} applied
to the theory itself: a posit is readable only where it is spent, and spending happens
in the direction of failure. Whether that is the deepest consistency or the most
elegant immunization is the one question this document cannot close --- and by its own
lights, should not be able to. Duty is the thing a life can only ever fail to prove.

The audit bottoms out here, for the framework's own reasons (\see{app:self-limit}): no
formalism contains the reading, no instrument reads the future at the instant --- so the
document, read, concludes its own audit has no formal close. This is not a weakness but
the shape of the thing: it stands on exactly two feet, the unreadable-now and the
settled-later, on a ground neither reaches.

\subsection{The wall, named}
\label{subsec:coda:wall}
\secgrade{nec}%
The genealogy has one shape because the wall has one name: economics is the
\textbf{variational sector} --- everything representable as motion on a posted potential,
$\gensym$'s whole world --- and this document is a theory of the remainder. The remainder
has two parts, and they part cleanly: the \textbf{skew sector}, non-variational and
readable (\ftag{dyn:loop}, \ftag{dyn:pitch}, \ftag{dyn:stand} --- the tremor that turns,
the spiral of neglect, the wave that travels), and the \textbf{will}, non-variational and
dark (\ftag{gen:will}, warranted in a private currency, \ftag{J:power}). One remainder you
can instrument; one you can only fund. The discipline's edge (\ftag{bd:edge}) is where
the second remainder holds up what the first cannot explain and the potential cannot pay
for. Each richer model relocates the residual; whether it \emph{closes} depends on the
metric, and the metric is the truth/flourishing distinction: a seminorm induced by
$\readout$ is blind to its own kernel --- residual measure-zero, converging \emph{to
invisibility}; a history-grounded metric sees it --- nonempty, bounded away. Both are
true; only the second is observer-independent. The record reads states; the flourishing
edge is the rate $\willvirtue$ that moves them --- the still center, out of scope to the
record.

\begin{econ}[label=e:capability, title={The flourishing pole}, ref=op:vmoral,
  cites={a:sencapability}, departs=annex,
  stopsat={capability is wellbeing as freedom-to-achieve, a state; virtue-as-enlargement is the rate that moves it, the information welfarism discards}]
  The flourishing pole is Sen's capability approach \parencite{a:sencapability}: wellbeing as
  freedom to achieve functionings, not utility and not the record. Virtue-as-enlargement is
  enlarged freedom-to-achieve, relational and unscalarizable --- exactly the information
  welfarism discards. The genealogy reads as one shape: economics builds
  outward from the funded interior and stops, each time, at a different face of one wall ---
  the input it cannot move, the aggregate that does not exist, the score that is a
  value-theory, the judge that is built not detached, the testimony it cannot de-colour, the
  cycle that rotates only when coupling is directed, the standing less aggregable than price,
  the edge that is a rate, the restraint priced only at the menu, the payroll it cannot
  total, the flourishing that is the history-metric.
\end{econ}

\subsection{The eight words}
\label{subsec:coda:words}
\secgrade{nec}%
What the document welded, gathered --- each word standing on the bone it was welded to:
\virtue{wonder} and \virtue{beauty}, the will that crosses before the reward exists and
what it finds on the far side; \virtue{goodness} and \virtue{kindness}, the enlargement
that raises real overlap, the second its quiet form at the modest good; \virtue{love},
the overlap drawing the self-boundary along held edges, the only rule that funds the
grenade; \virtue{wisdom}, the guard of the whole orthant boundary, refusing self-erasure
on one wall and exploitation on the other; \virtue{truth}, anchored legibility built
against the toll-keeper; and \virtue{health}, the self-floor, the boundary
self-deception is defined as crossing.

\subsection{The close}
\label{subsec:coda:close}
To love is to hold another's fate as partly your own; to be wise is to widen that holding
without erasing the holder. The judge everyone can trust, the reporter who favours no one, and
the self for whom every good act returns are not three but one, reached the one way the whole
field points: by raising $\comovfield$ toward all until your field is the whole field and you are enemy
to none --- folding every other's goods by every other's own measure --- at the price, in the
plural dimension of outcomes, of bearing all of it. And it can be counterfeited by anyone with
funding enough to look beloved, so the last thing the field can do is the first thing it most
needs: to tell the woven from the one only paid to seem so --- which settles, if ever, in
arrears, the only way unposted prices are ever read: spent, at a crossing, by other hands.

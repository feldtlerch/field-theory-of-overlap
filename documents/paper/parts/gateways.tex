\section{Independence and the Sybil wall}
\label{sec:anchor}

\subsection{Independence is not internally computed}
\label{subsec:anchor:internal}
\secgrade{cont-anch}%
The discount in $\effdensity$ is taken against the counterfactual defector (\ftag{f:defector})
--- but that reference profile is a node of the measured field, so
$\aligndefect{w}$ is itself an edge in it. With no external authority,
an adversary authors witnesses with overlap set to mimic independence.

\begin{theorem}[\hdrunbacked{The Anchor Joint}{J:anchor}]\grade{cont-anch}
  \begin{equation}
    \boxed{\ \effdensity\ \text{is not internally computable --- a regulative target
      from an exogenous anchor.}\ }
    \label{eq:J:anchor}
  \end{equation}
\end{theorem}

A funded Sybil farm, a clannish clique, and a sealed commons are one object: bought
co-movement internally indistinguishable from independent witnessing. The gap is detection,
not description. And the fabrication is single-authored --- staging the witness and staging
the co-movement it reports are one act, so a forger cannot stage the stake while leaving the
witnessing independently honest. The one combination an attacker would most want is the one
the constraint forbids.

A second independence, not yet payable here, waits on the report algebra: witnesses may be
independently authored and still all carry one slant. The copy-confound is killed by
$\effdensity$; the clique-confound is killed later by colour-span $\colourspan$ (\ftag{pr:span}).
Both are anchor debts, but they are not the same debt.

\subsection{The two overlaps}
\label{subsec:anchor:overlaps}
\secgrade{nec}%
The sign of the wall is a split \ftag{g:split} of $\alignfact$:
\begin{equation}
  \alignfact=\alignint+\alignanch.
  \label{eq:anchor:split}
\end{equation}
\handle{nrm:int} is within one author's power, fabricable on command; \handle{nrm:anch} is
grounded in an external process the adversary cannot author, or in non-collusion of
independent authors. Only \ftag*{nrm:anch} resists capture; the two are not interchangeable,
and every downstream use of $\effdensity$ inherits \gradeword{cont-anch} from this line.

\subsection{The second foot}
\label{subsec:anchor:second-foot}
\secgrade{nec}%
Crossing this gateway changes the modality of everything after it: downstream results are
\gradeword{cont-anch}, true within a shared anchor and null across one. This plants the second
terminus \handle{snk:anch}, the dual of the kernel filled at \ftag{J:occ}. Where \ftag{snk:ker}
is what no instrument reads \emph{now} --- the rate at rest --- \ftag*{snk:anch} is what no
instrument settles \emph{now} but time may settle later: the anchor, in arrears.

This sink itself splits \ftag{g:split}: \handle{snk:anch-t}, warrant-in-arrears (temporal,
axis $\dimtime$, settled later because downstream), and \handle{snk:anch-G}, anchor-relative
settlement (modal, contingent on a shared $\anchormetric$). In-world verdicts (\ftag{nrm:wise})
carry both; the document's own type-posits carry only \ftag*{snk:anch-t} --- settled-later without
being anchor-contingent, the timing/anchor split run one level up, as alignment/stake was run
on the operator. The fabrication line \ftag{bd:fab}, the forward leg of accountability, the
verdict that is real only once readers share a ground --- all settle here or never. The
framework has two feet, and they are these: the unreadable-now and the settled-later.

\section{Weight is not overlap}
\label{sec:hip}

\subsection{The three-way split}
\label{subsec:hip:split}
\secgrade{nec}%
A normative reading assigns a weight,
\begin{equation}
  U_i=\sum_j w_{ij}\,u_j,\qquad w_{ij}\ \text{free},
  \label{nrm:assign}
\end{equation}
and \ftag{g:split} at the hip holds apart five objects a single ``caring'' had fused.

\begin{theorem}[\hdr{J:hip}]\grade{nec}
  Five objects, not one:
  \par\medskip
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} l l L @{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{object} & \textbf{symbol} & \textbf{role} \\
    \midrule
    own measure & $\selfweight{i}$ & holder's private weighting \\
    holding & $\holding{i}{j}$ & edge belongs by positive aligned coupling \\
    assigned weight & $w_{ij}$ & free normative assignment (\ftag{nrm:assign}) \\
    currency & $\currency{i}{j}$ & convert $\dimnodewelfare{j}$ to $\dimnodewelfare{i}$ \\
    other's measure & $\feelingcovector{j}$ & content folded by the other's own measure \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \par\medskip
\end{theorem}

This is the plane the audit polices for the rest of the document --- $w$ against
$u,\leanbare,\selfweight{i}$ --- and the source of the anti-sacrificial structure
\ftag{nrm:self}.

\begin{definition}[\hdr{nrm:public}]\grade{nec}
  \begin{equation}
    \selfweight{i}\transpose\outcomevector{j}
    \quad/\quad
    \currency{i}{j}\selfweight{j}\transpose\outcomevector{j}
    \quad/\quad
    \normG{\outcomechange{j}}.
    \label{eq:nrm:public}
  \end{equation}
  These are annexation, inclusion, and publicization. The first two read the standing
  outcome $\outcomevector{j}$; only the anchored read is event-typed, in $\outcomechange{j}$.
\end{definition}

\subsection{The cartel is entailed, not added}
\label{subsec:hip:cartel}
\secgrade{nec}%
Set $w=\alignfact$ and the conflation has a cost it cannot avoid: for an insider
$j$ and an outsider $k$, the agent abets harm to $k$ whenever
\begin{equation}
  \assignweight{i}{j}\,\normof{\outcomechange{j}}>\assignweight{i}{k}\,\normof{\outcomechange{k}}.
  \label{eq:hip:cartel}
\end{equation}
Warmest in-group and cruelest cartel are one equation in two settings --- the harm is
\emph{entailed} by any weighting keyed to closeness, not added as a separate vice. Care may
set what you value and which edges you build; it may never discount a harm.

The directed operator sharpens the diagnosis twice. Collapsing a person's plural memberships
into one in-group licenses out-group harm --- the $w=\leanbare$ cartel. And folding the other
under one's \emph{own} weighting $\selfweight{i}$ rather than theirs --- annexation
\ftag{nrm:annex} --- is the same collapse run inside care itself, the cartel of love. The
asymmetry is dimensional, not only moral: folding the other by their own measure
$\privatewelfare{j}$ requires the currency that makes their welfare-length commensurable
with yours, while folding by your own caring covector
$\caringcovector{i}{j}\transpose\outcomevector{j}$ lands in your units unaided ---
annexation is unit-closed, inclusion is currency-dependent, and the cartel of love is the
path of least resistance in the units as well as the heart. Both are one \ftag{g:split}
failure: a weighting keyed to closeness, applied where only the other's measure belongs.

  drawn at one wall instead of along the edges actually held.
\end{econ}

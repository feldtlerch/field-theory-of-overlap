\subsection{No dimensionally-natural norm}
\label{subsec:prof:nonorm}
\secgrade{nec}%
\begin{theorem}[\hdr{P:nonorm}]\grade{nec}
  The profile $\profile$ spans three incommensurable dimensions, and its narration coordinate
  is the node-private welfare-like axis fixed at \ftag{P:vec}, so the agency-score fails twice
  over, on independent grounds:
  \begin{equation}
    \normof{\profile}^2\sim\dimratio+\dimratio+\dimrate\sp{2}+\dimrate\sp{2}
      +\dimnodewelfaresq{i}+\dimrate\sp{2}+\dimrate\sp{2},
    \label{eq:P:nonorm-expand}
  \end{equation}
  Public commensuration can produce $\dimwelfare$ for shared goods, but the profile coordinate
  written here is $\dimnodewelfare{i}$. The score is ill-posed across nodes without currency
  and type-incorrect \emph{between} axes, and these are separable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{statement}{P:axes}{Within-axis and between-axis}
  $\anchormetric$ resolves the within-axis incommensurability; the between-axis wall
  $\dimwelfare$ vs.\ $\dimrate$ survives any $\anchormetric$.
\end{statement}

A community can share a complete $\anchormetric$ --- perfect agreement on how all goods weigh
--- and still have no agency-score, because no metric trades welfare-states against
will-rates. The space is a genuine dimensioned vector space: addition, dimensionless scaling,
coordinate projection, single-axis comparison, and the componentwise partial order are all
legal. Illegal is every \emph{scalarization} --- norm, inner product, distance, rank ---
exactly the operations that collapse the components into one number. It is \ftag{g:knock} on
the grandest sentence the document states. And the claim has a native literature:
representational measurement theory \parencite{a:kls} proves exactly these uniqueness and
scale-type results --- what is argued here as a type discipline stands there as theorems, and the
citation converts a rhetorical claim into a lineage.

\subsection{Every score smuggles an exchange-rate}
\label{subsec:prof:leak}
\secgrade{nec}%
A norm is not impossible to \emph{define} --- only definable by importing dimensioned
constants that make the axes commensurable: a time-horizon $\horizon\,\dimtime$ (will
$\leftrightarrow$ connection) and a welfare-scale $\welfarescale\,\dimwelfare$ (claim $\leftrightarrow$
connection). These are derivable from nothing; they \emph{are} a value-theory.

\begin{definition}[\hdrunbacked{The leaked exchange-rates}{P:leak}]\grade{nec}
  The framework leaks one such constant at every scalarization:
  \par\medskip
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} l l >{\hsize=1.4\hsize}L @{}}
    \toprule \textbf{constant} & \textbf{dim} & \textbf{trades} \\ \midrule
    \Wstar            & \dimwelfare         & rent $\leftrightarrow$ surplus \\
    \horizon          & \dimtime            & will $\leftrightarrow$ connection \\
    \welfarescale     & \dimwelfare         & claim $\leftrightarrow$ connection \\
    \countpool        & \dimcount           & witnesses $\leftrightarrow$ density \\
    \belongingradiusof{i} & \dimnodeperpublic{i} & inclusion's radius (the warmest) \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
  \par\medskip
\end{definition}

Demanding ``show me your conversion constants'' \emph{is} the refutation of any merit-rank,
eugenic score, or social-credit number: each is a posted $(\horizon,\welfarescale)$ wearing the mask of a
fact.

\begin{econ}[label=e:robbins, title={Robbins, dimensioned}, ref=P:nonorm,
  cites={a:robbins,a:fisher,a:capital}, departs=scalar,
  stopsat={imports interpersonal comparison as a methodological caution; here a type-signature, the gap found within one life twice}]
  \textcite{a:robbins}: interpersonal utility comparison has no scientific basis --- a value
  judgment imported, not derived; the index-number problem \parencite{a:fisher} is the same
  gap for incommensurable goods. The $(\horizon,\welfarescale)$ theorem is Robbins dimensioned --- and where
  Robbins located the gap \emph{between} persons, the same gap is found \emph{within} one life
  twice: within the outcome-dimension (dissolved by posting an anchored $\anchormetric$) and
  between the trait-dimensions (immune to any $\anchormetric$, the antichain surviving a
  perfect commensuration of goods). The capital controversy \parencite{a:capital} is the gap
  found \emph{within one aggregate}; the framework carries all three faces and gives it a
  type-signature rather than a methodological caution.
\end{econ}

\subsection{At home, too}
\label{subsec:prof:athome}
\secgrade{nec}%
Non-normability is not a cross-species exotic. Within one species, four pairwise-incomparable
lives --- the faithful inarticulate carer (high $\traitwill$, low $\traitnarration$), the brilliant
manipulator (high $\traitdirection/\traitnarration$, low restraint), the charismatic builder ($+\traitbuilding$), the
lucid addict --- and the decisive case is the dissociation
\begin{statement}{P:akrasia}{The akratic dissociation}
  $\traitnarration\perp \traitwill$: self-narration orthogonal to maintenance --- self-awareness
  without the power to hold.
\end{statement}
This is akrasia, the visible face of a non-normable profile: the coordinates carry
incommensurable dimensions, one ($\traitrestraint$) is unreadable, and no order survives.
Non-normability holds at home as cleanly as across the kingdoms.

\refrain{}{A sign read at home --- in your own units, by your own weighting --- of whether the other's good comes home at all; that much is felt. Where the line falls for everyone at once is anchored; where the gift tips is anchored twice; and what no crowd can ever supply is the one access that would settle it.}

\subsection{The dignity landing}
\label{subsec:prof:dignity}
\secgrade{nec}%
The sparse profile (the infant, the disabled, the demented) is not a lesser
\emph{quantity} of person --- there is no quantity: \ftag{P:nonorm} is the type-check, not
a low score. And by the self-funding self, such a profile is \textbf{dense in the held edges
of others}: inclusion folds each member by \emph{their} holder's measure (\ftag{nrm:incl}, the
aligned stake), and for the infant that stake is large --- caring aligns with need, intensely
--- so the components others hold \emph{with} them are real, in-dimension, and large. Dignity
rides being held, not holding; non-normability and the extended self are one result, read
twice. A life is a place, and the place is woven of others' holdings.

\refrain{}{The agency-score was never a number. There was nothing to argue down --- only a
price someone hoped to hide, and the dimensions will not let it stay hidden.}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent A relationship is not mainly a feeling. It is a pattern of co-movement: if
something happens to one life, something happens to another. This explainer gives the
plain-language path through the formal paper (\docdraft, \docversion). It opens with a
summary in ordinary words --- no tags, no symbols --- and then walks the paper's path,
keeping its central unit checks in view: direction is not magnitude, a state is not a
rate, what could move is not what did move, a crowd is not independence, a fact is not a
verdict, and a life is not a score. The record can be given better and better
instruments, and the theory welcomes every one; it also says exactly what the best
instrument still misses: not the work, which trembles visibly, but what the work is paid
in.
\end{abstract}

One cell deserves its footnote: sacrifice, the bottom-left corner. Draining your life to
help someone who is genuinely part of your life --- a child, a friend held close --- is
not finally a drain; the help comes home, and the act belongs to the bright square after
all. Draining yourself for a stranger is generous, admirable, and the theory will insist
on calling it exactly what it is: good, and not wise. Both halves of that sentence
matter, and neither cancels the other.

Sometimes a martyr is called for --- but that already means we've left the ideal, where
helping and thriving no longer pull apart. When the sacrifice truly reaches someone, the
framework calls it good; when it's for a stranger, it cannot also call it wise --- and
it means both words at once, honoring the act without recommending it as a way to live.

\textbf{Witnesses.}\enspace Nobody sees an event from nowhere. Every account of what
happened comes from someone who stands somewhere --- nearer the doer or nearer the
done-to --- and the account carries that slant along with the fact. You cannot fix slant
with volume. A hundred retellings of one account are still one account; a hundred
witnesses who all share one slant give you no way to tell the fact from the slant. What
pins a fact down is difference: independent witnesses whose slants oppose. When the
doer's friend and the done-to's friend say the same thing --- each from their own
seeing, not from one retold story --- that thing is probably what happened. That is
also all the scientific method is, in this vocabulary: make a claim
cheap to check, let people of every slant check it, and keep what survives. Events open
to that checking can be settled globally, for everyone. Judgments of persons, we will
see, cannot be settled so --- and the difference between those two sentences is most of
this theory. The
same logic picks out a trustworthy judge --- and it is not the judge with no
attachments. A judge tied to no one is unbiased and useless: no one has any reason to
believe them. The trustworthy judge is tied to all sides evenly --- fair by balance, not
by absence.

\textbf{Scores.}\enspace Acts can be recorded. Persons cannot be graded. A life has many
kinds of measure in it --- how entangled, how witnessed, how hard-holding, how
self-aware, how restrained --- and these do not share a unit, any more than temperature
and distance do. Any single life-score therefore smuggles in exchange rates somebody
chose: so many units of talent per unit of kindness. That choice is a moral judgment
wearing the costume of arithmetic. Ask anyone offering to rank people one question ---
\emph{show me your conversion rates} --- and the ranking unravels on its own. The paper
takes its own medicine here: every exchange rate it uses is declared on a public
ledger, and every result carries a grade saying what kind of rate it leans on. Where a
result is written in the paper's checked notation, it cannot claim to lean on less than
it does; elsewhere the grade is the author's word, open to inspection.

\textbf{What to build.}\enspace So: record acts, never persons. Price what an act
actually moved in other lives --- both directions, help and harm. Note which way ties
lean, but never charge anyone for a lean. Publish the measuring rod the log itself reads
by, so the log's own slant sits on the record where every other slant sits. Believe
facts when independent, differently-slanted witnesses converge on them. Watch the
trusted most, because trust is where betrayal pays best. Ask; never compel. And build no
total score --- the total that would travel across communities is the one number the
whole construction proves empty.

\begin{statement}{xstmt:plain}{One breath}
  Price what moves; log which way it leans; publish your own measuring rod; believe
  independent, differently-slanted witnesses; watch the trusted; never total a person.
\end{statement}

% generated: sec:explainer:edge
\begin{statement}{xstmt:comovement}{Co-movement}
  A relationship is not mainly a feeling. It is a pattern of co-movement.
\end{statement}

If something happens to you, does something happen to me? If your loss becomes partly my
loss, if your danger changes my future, then there is an edge between us. The paper
calls that edge an \gloss{operator}{a rule that turns a change over there into a change
over here}, written $\comov{i}{j}$: a map from changes in your goods to changes in mine.
Concretely: if the mill upstream burns, the bakery's week changes --- that is
co-movement, whatever the baker feels about the miller, and even if they have never met.

One edge, and the paper reads it four ways, each with its own unit.

First, \textbf{alignment} --- and it comes in two tenses. The \emph{standing} lean asks:
do our fates lean together, on the whole? The \emph{event} lean asks: did this
particular act lean with me or against me? Both are \gloss{cosines}{pure
direction-numbers between $-1$ and $1$, with all size divided out}, and they need not
agree: a well-leaning friend can land an ill-leaning act.

Second, \textbf{capacity}: how much \emph{could} move through this edge --- the width of
the channel, act or no act. Only the genuine stranger has zero capacity; capacity is
what makes you answerable at all.

Third, the \textbf{charge}: how much an actual act \emph{did} move, measured on the
public rod. Gate on capacity; charge on realization; never swap them --- and never
charge on the lean, which either party can dissolve simply by re-weighing what they care
about.

Fourth, the \textbf{felt total}: what the event cost or paid \emph{you, by your own
caring}. This is the quiet marvel among the four: it needs no shared measuring rod at
all. What an act cost its victim is a fact prior to any community's units --- two
communities can disagree about an event's size and about its lean and still agree what
it cost the one it landed on. \emph{And must --- once the sufferer's own weighing is in
hand}: the total is built on no shared rod, but it is read through the holder's own, so
what the communities share is the invariant, not free access to it. How the felt total
decomposes into
lean-times-size is anchored, negotiable, community-relative; that it happened, and its
sign --- helped or hurt --- is not.

The paper works all four readings through one tiny worked world --- two neighbours, two
goods, bread and song --- where a friend's unambiguously good day (more bread, more
song) lands as a loss on the neighbour whose own singing it drowns out: standing lean
mildly positive, event lean negative, felt total negative, capacity untouched. Every
confusion the four readings prevent appears in that toy at hand-calculable scale.

\section{Goods, Converters, and the One Fact}
\label{sec:explainer:goods}

The theory insists that goods are plural. Health, dignity, money, status, safety,
belonging: these are not automatically one thing. They are
\gloss{incommensurable}{not measurable on one shared scale without someone choosing the
scale}. To add them, someone must supply a converter. A person has a private weighting
$\selfweight{i}$ --- held, lived, and partly dark even to its holder. A public system
may post a \gloss{metric}{a rule for measuring lengths and comparing unlike goods}
$\anchormetric$ --- \gloss{anchored}{fixed by something outside the system it is used to
measure}, argued for, and openly anchor-dependent. There is no third option, and no view
from nowhere that turns all goods and all lives into one natural number.

The paper grounds all of this in one epistemic principle, stated as a posit and then
made to earn its keep:

\begin{statement}{xstmt:converter}{The converter principle}
  A converter is readable only where it is spent.
\end{statement}

Any exchange rate between unlike things --- a weighting, a currency, a metric --- shows
itself exactly at its transactions, and nowhere else. Watch what someone actually trades
and you can recover the rates they traded at; a rate never spent into any transaction is
not hidden knowledge waiting for a better instrument --- there is nothing on record to
read, by the nature of the case.

This is why the paper keeps doing unit checks --- the physicist's habit of refusing to
add metres to degrees. A welfare state is not a rate. A direction is not a magnitude. A
crowd is not independence. A life is not a score. And one fact organizes the rest:

\begin{statement}{xstmt:fact}{The one fact}
  A state is readable at a point; a rate is not.
\end{statement}

A \emph{state} is how things stand --- a balance, a temperature, a photograph. A
\emph{rate} is how hard something is being pushed --- and no single instant shows it.
The fact is the shadow the converter principle casts on the poorest possible instrument,
the single passive snapshot; richer instruments read more, and the principle says
exactly how much more. A time series reads persistence; a fluctuation record reads
maintenance --- the trembling of the held glass; a probe reads the restoring push. Each
rung of that ladder removes from the dark precisely what is spent into it, and nothing
that is not. The ladder never reaches the unspent converter. That is the theory's map of
ignorance: not a lament that we cannot know, but an exact accounting of which darkness
is an instrument problem and which is a fact about the world.

\paperrefrain{}{A snapshot shows the glass is still. The film shows it
trembling --- and still cannot show what the holding costs the hand.}

\PaperParagraph{lastline}That image is central. The theory says much of moral life is like the hand holding the
glass still.

\section{Will as Rate}
\label{sec:explainer:will}

The will in this system is not a vague soul-word. It is a \textbf{rate}, $\will$: a
continuous expenditure of effort --- and specifically, effort that can oppose the
\gloss{reward gradient}{the direction in which payoff increases fastest; the way the
incentives point}. If money, comfort, status, or power pull one way, the will is what
can move against the pull: build a tie reward would not fund, maintain fidelity against
loosening, restrain a harm that would have paid, reconcile an enmity, or sever a bad
bond. The friendship kept alive though nothing pays for it; the apology that costs
status --- these are the will in its native register.

The will is also \emph{signed}. Its ledger contains an anti-will: effort spent to keep a
gap open because the gap pays --- the toll-keeper funding the wall between two
communities his brokerage lives on. Do not confuse that with decay. Decay is friction,
nobody's budget; the anti-will is a budget, somebody's spend. A tie that merely lapsed
and a tie that is being actively held apart look alike from outside, and they are
different diseases.

Second, the decay constant is not a posted number. On the chosen branch, fading is
\emph{derived from wobbling}: the same restlessness that makes a held tie tremble makes
a neglected tie fade, and one constant governs both. Measure how held ties tremble and
you have predicted how abandoned ties die --- one fewer exchange rate for a value theory
to hide in, and one new way to catch the theory lying.

Where a tie loosens when left alone --- and most do --- some become self-reinforcing
once enough surplus and witness density are present; below that
\gloss{threshold}{a tipping point past which a process feeds itself instead of dying
out} nothing starts by itself. Note what the threshold does \emph{not} contain: the
will. The will seeds a crossing; it does not set the bar.

This gives the theory one of its deepest claims:

\begin{statement}{xstmt:fidelity}{Fidelity is standing work}
  Fidelity is not a frozen state. If an edge stays alive despite decay, and no reward
  feeds it, something is
  continuously holding it --- in expectation, at every moment; where reward and decay do
  not balance on their own, the residue is someone's standing work. The film can even
  watch
  it tremble. What no record shows is the wage: by what private weighing the holder
  counts this edge worth the holding.
\end{statement}

So there is a hidden region: the \gloss{kernel}{the part of reality an instrument maps
to zero --- its built-in blind spot}, the still center. The paper counts \textbf{seven
tenants}. Five are occupied --- a standing rate held on a slope, work, dark: restraint
(the declined harm that would have paid), fidelity (the held edge), severance-resolve
(the will to leave, held against an attachment that pulls toward staying), self-fidelity
(the holder's own weighting, kept against its own drift --- the inward tenant, dark even
to its holder), and toll-keeping (the anti-will's tenancy: the same silence that keeps
the faithful keeps the rent-guard). One room is genuinely empty: lapse, the good left
undone with no push either way --- rest, not restraint; the paper's word is
\emph{insufficiency}, neither vice nor virtue, but not nothing to regret. And one tenant
straddles the doorway: duty kept from bind rather than pull, whose acting phase is
public and judged, and whose source is dark in a deeper way than all the others --- not
a rate too still to read, but a thing with no units at all.

\section{Good and Wise}
\label{sec:explainer:good-wise}

The paper separates \textbf{good} from \textbf{wise}. Goodness is the present effect on
another: did this act help or harm the subject, now? Wisdom is the forward effect on the
actor's extended self: did it preserve or enlarge the self that must live onward? Both
are welfare readings; they differ in time-index. Neither is the will, which is a rate.
In the opening grid (\zcref{xtbl:L}), the columns are goodness; the rows, once the self
is properly extended, are wisdom.

And the two are not symmetrically yours. The column is chosen now; the row settles
later, partly by the network, \gloss{in arrears}{settled after the fact, like a debt
paid late}. Helping settles into enlargement or martyrdom; harming settles into
exploitation-that-paid or spite-that-backfired. The act is half-chosen and half-fated,
and the row cannot be read at the instant it is authored.

\paperrefrain{}{You author the act; the network authors the response.}

The extended self is one of the most beautiful ideas here. You do not expand yourself by
announcing that everyone matters. You expand yourself by actually holding edges ---
making another's good come home to you as partly your own. And the other must be folded
in \emph{by their own measure}, not yours: forcing your values over their life is
annexation, the cartel run inside care --- the parent who ``gives everything'' to a
child while deciding, unilaterally, what the child is to want. The paper notes, dryly,
that annexation is also the path of least resistance in the arithmetic: folding the
other by your own weighting needs no currency conversion, while honoring their measure
requires the harder work of exchange --- the cartel of love is cheaper in the units as
well as the heart.

That matters because closeness can become a cartel. If I weight my friends' welfare
higher simply because they are close, I may excuse harm to outsiders --- the warm
in-group and the cruel cartel are one inequality in two settings, the harm
\emph{entailed} by any closeness-keyed weighting, not added as a separate vice. Care may
guide where I invest myself. It may never make another person's harm count for less.

So the theory is anti-selfish but also anti-self-erasure. Sacrifice for someone truly
inside the held self can be wise; sacrifice for a stranger may be good but not wise ---
the contested bottom-left corner of the opening grid, resolved exactly as the paper
resolves it: the grenade fallen on for a child held close is wise, its discriminator a
standing state, present-tense; the martyr for strangers is good and not wise, and the
resolution is not to forbid the martyr but to widen holding until the stranger is not
one. Goodness can exceed wisdom --- that is not a failure; it is one of the ways humans
are luminous. The paper's own weld: the holding that draws the self-boundary along held
edges is \papervirtue{love}; the guard of the whole boundary --- refusing self-erasure
on one wall and exploitation on the other --- is \papervirtue{wisdom}.

\paperrefrain{}{You become good not by widening the circle in name, but by
extending the real boundary of the self outward, edge by edge.}

\section{Self-Deception and the L}
\label{sec:explainer:the-L}

Return to the opening grid (\zcref{xtbl:L}) and its backwards L: the harmed column, the
drained row. The paper's account of self-deception is built directly on it.

Every person carries a \gloss{readout}{the map from what actually happened to what gets
recorded --- by a ledger, or by your own memory and self-story}. Self-deception is not
random noise on that map. It is a specific corruption: the warm feeling that genuine
virtue earns is \emph{installed} over an L-act, so that exploitation reads, from inside,
as enlargement --- and self-erasure reads as love. The counterfeit does not invent a new
feeling; it spends the true one.

The consoling half is a hard bound: the corrupted readout and the honest one agree
everywhere \emph{except} the L. In the bright square there is nothing to hide, so
nothing gets falsified. The bleak thought --- \emph{you can never know whether you are
good} --- over-reaches. What is true is that you cannot \emph{score} how good you are,
because there was never a number; it does not follow that something is being hidden from
you. Honest love is uncertain of its fine grain, not deceived about its cell.

The uncomfortable half: virtue and its counterfeit run on the same three coins ---
trust, the record, and warm feeling. Every instrument sharp enough to detect the fake is
also a resource the fake can spend. And beneath the whole layer sits the philosophers'
old point, which the paper meets rather than fights --- the one citation in its whole
genealogy it does not depart from: a web of belief can always be adjusted to protect any
single claim --- \gloss{underdetermination}{the evidence alone does not force one
reading; something else must choose}. The funded fake is exactly that adjustment, with a
budget --- and the funded fake returns two sections below, wearing its most dangerous
costume.

The paper ranks its darknesses, and the ranking is worth carrying. Shallowest: the
moving flux anyone recovers by watching long enough. Beneath it: the held weighting at

\section{Witnesses}
\label{sec:explainer:witnesses}

A report is not just a fact. It is a fact plus \gloss{colour}{the slant a witness's own
stakes put on their report --- who they lean toward, times how much they stand to lose}:
a witness leans toward the actor, the victim, both, or neither, and their testimony
carries content and slant together. A reader can subtract slant only if they can read
the witness's position --- so credibility is reader-relative. And the most motivated
witness is, by construction, the actor's enemy --- which makes them the least
de-colourable to exactly the actor's own side. The victim's true report is discounted
precisely where it most needs hearing --- by geometry, not malice; the silencing of
victims is derived here, not assumed.

The cure is not ``more witnesses.'' A crowd can be one copied voice, or one shared slant
--- and those are different diseases with different cures. Independence --- not authored
by one hand; the \gloss{Sybil problem}{one author minting many fake witnesses that look
like a crowd} is why independence can never be computed from inside the system --- kills
the copies. \textbf{Colour-span} --- witnesses of different slants whose reports bracket
the fact --- kills the cliques. If the actor's friend and the victim's friend
independently converge, that is powerful; a hundred accounts of one colour cannot
separate the fact from the slant, and the paper proves the point as arithmetic: the
information a crowd carries about the bare fact is its count \emph{times its span}. A
single-slant crowd, however vast, multiplies by zero.

\paperrefrain{}{A crowd is not enough; the witnesses must be independent --- a
crowd buys down idiosyncrasy and stops at the shared blind spot, and only an outside
anchor moves the cap.}

This machinery has a bright side, and the paper dwells on it: some things \emph{can} be
settled globally. When a claim is cheap to check --- anyone can re-run the test,
whatever they lean toward --- it recruits witnesses across the whole spectrum of slants,
and when opposed slants converge on one answer, the slant has been subtracted and what
remains is the fact. That is the scientific method, read in this vocabulary:
\gloss{reproducibility}{independent parties re-querying the world and getting the same
answer} is colour-span with the world itself as the anchor. Proof-of-work is the same
corner engineered: costly to produce, cheap to verify, so every colour can adjudicate it
--- the burned cost measures no virtue and certifies no verdict; it buys the chance that
witnesses of more than one colour will look. So the theory is not skepticism, and the
wall it builds later is not a wall against knowledge: facts travel.

\begin{statement}{xstmt:sweep}{Fact and verdict}
  A fact is what survives the colour sweep. A verdict is the colour sweep.
\end{statement}

The same logic picks the trustworthy judge --- and it is not the detached one.
Detachment is unbiased and worthless: colour-zero by absence, credible to no one. The
ideal judge is \textbf{equi-interested}: close to all parties, enemy to none,
colour-zero by \emph{balance} of attachments. Impartiality is not the view from nowhere;
it is the view from everywhere --- an ideal built edge by edge, and in a divided network
the seat may stand empty: no candidate reaches everyone, and that vacancy is exactly
what standing institutions exist to fill.

% generated: sec:say:enemy-to-none

But this ideal can be forged --- here is the funded fake again, in its most dangerous
costume. A funded actor can build a beloved, apparently neutral authority over a
concealed dependence. What the forgery steals is not credibility but
\emph{de-colourability}: the genuine judge is faithful to all; the forged one shows the
same zero over a hidden slant no reader can subtract, because it is concealed. The saint
and the paid saint share one surface; only anchors, independence, and time separate them
--- in arrears.

The third rung is the network claim. In the paper's simplest witnessing model ---
reports are instantaneous, and nothing in a tie stores momentum --- symmetric witnessing
can \emph{stand} in any pattern: everyone rising and falling together, or neighbours
frozen in alternation. What it can never do is \emph{circulate}. A wave of
opening-and-closing travelling around a loop of communities, with no change in
fundamentals, would prove part of the model false --- and the paper names the exits in
advance: either the witnessing is directed (flowing one way around the loop, like rumour
along a one-way street), or the reports carry a delay, or the ties themselves store
momentum. Hold the last two shut, and a travelling wave certifies directed witnessing.
That is a \gloss{falsifiable}{the theory names, in advance, an observation that would
prove it wrong} statement about real networks. And the rungs are not redundant: the
first two read the turn \emph{inside} one tie, the third the turn \emph{between} ties
--- so a mismatch across scales is not noise but a location: it tells you where the
turning lives. A theory that only refused things would be safe; this one predicts, in
triplicate, with a cross-check.

\section{Verdicts and Lives}
\label{sec:explainer:verdicts-lives}

Now the hard claim, stated with its own care:

\begin{statement}{xstmt:verdict}{No portable verdict}
  There is no global verdict on a person that survives the crossing of anchors.
\end{statement}

There can be local verdicts: given my stake, my access, my de-colouring of the reports,
this act matters this way \emph{to me}. Within a community that shares an anchor and a
\gloss{commensuration}{a shared rule for comparing unlike goods --- an agreed answer to
``how much health per unit of dignity?''}, those verdicts can genuinely agree. But the
portable scalar --- ``safe,'' ``dangerous,'' ``high score'' --- is the component the
paper proves carries zero information across anchors: the aggregate is the projection
onto the \emph{kernel} of the signal --- the direction along which the data carry
nothing at all.

Hold this against the previous section, because the asymmetry is the whole point, and
the paper proves it twice, from two sides. The epistemic side: a reader-independent
de-colouring would need exactly the external anchor no internal computation supplies, so
the global scalar is unidentifiable. The ontic side: a fact has a world behind it ---
strip away every witness's slant and a remainder is left standing, which is why opposed
labs can converge, and why cross-colour agreement can make the whole world agree on
\emph{what happened}. A verdict has no world behind it; it is proportional to the
reader's own stake, so stripping the slant strips the verdict --- the perfectly detached
reader's verdict is not unknown but exactly zero. The same machinery that settles facts
globally proves the person-score empty: what the colour sweep leaves of an event is the
event; what it leaves of a judgment is nothing. Science and the null verdict are one
result read from its two sides. The act can be logged. The person cannot be certified.
And note the register: this is not a prohibition anyone is being scolded about; the
certificate is a category error, an empty component --- there is no throne here to
refuse, because the throne was never load-bearing.

The same logic applies to lives. A life has coordinates --- entanglement,
witnessability, maintenance, direction, narration, institution-building, restraint ---
carrying incommensurable dimensions. No natural norm crosses them: like taking one
Euclidean length over temperature, velocity, love, and dollars. You can invent
conversion rates, but then you must admit what you did --- you posted a value theory.
Demanding ``show me your conversion constants'' \emph{is} the refutation of every
merit-rank, eugenic score, and social-credit number. Even triage, the hardest case,
obeys the type-check: when triage compares two patients, the chooser's own units cancel
out of the comparison --- what remains is a posted rate \emph{between the others},
someone's choice, never a discovered height of persons.

\paperrefrain{}{When triage reaches for worth, it finds no height of self ---
only a posted rate between the others, which someone hoped to read as fate.}

\begin{statement}{xstmt:place}{A life is a place}
  A life is a place, not a height.
\end{statement}

This protects dignity. The infant, the demented elder, the silent animal, the
inarticulate carer --- none can be placed on a natural ladder of more-person and
less-person; the dimensions refuse the score. The paper shows the refusal at home as
well as across the kingdoms: the strangler vine, the brokering fungus, the
dialect-holding whale, the language-reared ape --- four lives, none dominating another
--- and, within one species, the faithful inarticulate carer against the brilliant
manipulator, with akrasia as the visible proof that self-awareness and the power to hold
are different axes. And a sparse profile is not an empty life: a being may be richly
held in the edges of others. Dignity rides being held, not only holding.

argue down --- only a price someone hoped to hide, and the dimensions will not let it
stay hidden.}

\section{The Signed Externality Log}
\label{sec:explainer:log}

What, then, can we build? One artifact: a \textbf{signed externality log}. Record acts,
both valences --- harm and help. Price their charge --- what actually moved in other
lives, read on a rod the log itself publishes: the log's own measuring axis is posted
per entry, criticizable, its lean on the record where every other lean is. Gate on
capacity. Log alignment only as context, never as the basis of a charge. Anchor belief
in independence and colour-span. Build no global verdict --- none crosses the anchor
wall.

It needs four buildables: \textbf{record}, per-act and signed --- and stamped with
enough context (witness identity, a baseline on the tie) that later readers can subtract
the slant, since \emph{built the friendship} and \emph{were friends already} are
invisible from an endpoint alone; \textbf{belief}, kept deliberately as a pair ---
independence (not one author) and colour-span (not one slant), because copies and
cliques are different diseases, and only under a stated orthogonality may the pair be
multiplied into one number; \textbf{relevance}, each reader scaling facts by their own
stake; \textbf{salience}, a \gloss{sunk cost}{a cost paid up front and unrecoverable, so
it cannot be a bribe or a bet} that measures nothing but buys a chance that witnesses of
more than one colour will look --- deliberate recruitment of span where obviousness will
not recruit it for free.

And it stands on three refusals --- the paper's, not paraphrases:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{No stake}: the log itself must hold no stake in any outcome --- which is
  exactly what makes it de-colourable by everyone.
\item \textbf{Watch the trusted most}: trust withdraws surveillance, and a predator's
  best runway is a trusted position; equal watching under-watches where arbitrage pays.
\item \textbf{Ask, never compel}: compulsion manufactures records correlated with the
  compeller --- worthless --- and stakes the log. Facts about an act may be compelled
  and cross-examined; a standing edge may not.
\end{itemize}

Two honest costs are named rather than hidden. The cut is always a wound: every
cost-anchor that excludes the costless bot excludes the genuine poor by the same coin.
And justice is built reach: good deeds self-witness; harm needs standing institutions
that hold paths open for the severed --- more, that \emph{manufacture} the
equi-interested witness no victim can be expected to be, giving the role the balance
disinterest lacks and the motivation pay-and-duty supply.

And the log runs on four wills it cannot build: someone must fill it; someone must look
from outside; the keepers must refuse to become what the log would make them --- the
broker over every edge, the highest-rent capture available, refused in an act of
restraint only the keepers fully understand, the one silence in the paper that is a
refusal rather than an absence; and someone must seed the frame before any reward for
doing so exists. A ledger is a body; these are its pulse.

The surveillance machine is this same artifact with the safeguards removed: stake added,
compulsion added, and the null verdict sold as truth.

the global verdict --- and the global verdict was never real.}

\section{The Paper and Its Checks}
\label{sec:explainer:typed}

The demand this theory makes of every score --- \emph{show me your conversion rates} ---
is made of the paper itself, at every build, as far as its typed notation reaches.

Every quantity written in the paper's typed notation is declared once, with its units
and with the list of posted rates it spends. Every result carries a grade: a result that
spends no converter is marked as standing on construction alone; a result that leans on
the public metric is marked as anchored, true within a community that shares the rod; a
result that leans on someone's private weighting is marked as settling only in that
holder's own biography, in arrears. Where its quantities are typed, two of these are
\emph{checked}, not merely asserted: mark a result as standing on construction alone when it records any spend, or as anchored
when it leans on a held or openly owed one, and the document is refused at typesetting. The exchange rates themselves sit in one generated
ledger --- posted, held, or openly owed --- rendered from the same declarations the
spends are checked against, so a typed debt cannot hide at a call site; a judgment call
the checker had to make would be published as a ruling rather than smuggled, and none
is posted; and the fork in the dynamics is a priced menu, so nobody inherits the chosen
branch without seeing what the other branches would have cost.

And the paper states the limit of its own trick, in the same breath: the build
certifies \emph{consistency with the declarations}, never the declarations' truth.
Spends are self-reported; a determined author could lie to the machine. Nor does the
checker reach everything: the flagship dynamics displays are set outside the typed
notation and pass unchecked, and so does ordinary algebra in the prose. Beyond that,
the build reproduces both documents byte for byte and resolves every tag and
reference; whether the reported spends are true and complete, and whether the argument
holds, is for the reader. What the machinery buys is compression --- the whole audit
surface reduced to one screen a skeptical reader can check in an afternoon --- which is
precisely the paper's own doctrine applied to itself: settlement in arrears, by other
hands.

\section{The Two Feet, and the Ground}
\label{sec:explainer:two-feet}

At the end, the whole theory stands on two feet --- and names what they stand on. The
feet are two tenses of one verb, \emph{spend}. The first is the
\textbf{spent-privately}: the kernel, where rates are held at rest and converters are
spent into no public record --- the hand on the glass, readable, if ever, through the
holder's own biography. The second is the \textbf{not-yet-spent}: the anchor, where
time, independence, and cross-colour convergence may eventually sort fact from forgery
--- in arrears. There is no third foot. But there is a third tense, and it is not a
foot; it is the \textbf{ground}: the unspendable --- the converter spent into no
currency at all, the duty kept from bind rather than pull. That is not slow to read.
There is nothing to read, by type. The record's instruments climb their ladder ---
persistence, trembling, release, circulation --- and every rung removes something from
the dark and nothing from this residue. A well-founded darkness is not a retreating
shadow; it is a fixed complement, and the paper can point to its exact boundary.

The paper also names the one wall its whole genealogy keeps hitting: economics is the
science of everything representable as motion on a posted landscape of rewards, and this
theory is a theory of the remainder. The remainder has two parts. One turns and can be
instrumented --- the tremor, the spiral, the wave. One holds and can only be funded ---
the will. One remainder you can watch; one you can only pay for.

## Small capitals (question 1)

Measured on H1 and H2, new this round: `html { font-synthesis: none }`, the handles are lowercase in the markup (`snk:anch-t`), computed `font-variant-caps: small-caps`, `font-synthesis: none`, `font-feature-settings: normal`; an embedded `Pagella` italic face (400, italic) is declared and loaded; `.math-tag` (links inside the formula) are `font-style: italic`, `.math-handle` and `.equation-number` (upright handles and "(22.1)") are `font-style: normal`.
- Font tables (parsed from the embedded data URLs): Pagella regular, bold and italic all have GSUB `smcp` and `c2sc`; Pagella Math has no `smcp`.
- Widths in the DOM against a canvas measurement of the same face and size (canvas `fontVariantCaps`): italic handle `snk:anch-t` at 9.867 px measured 46.56 px; the expectation for real italic small caps is 46.59, for lowercase 41.36, for full capitals 59.69, so it is true small capitals, neither lowercase nor full capitals nor 0.8 times the capitals (0.8 x 59.69 = 47.8 would be wrong by 1.2 px, and the canvas small-cap width 472.2 per 100 px against 484 for scaled capitals). H2 italic `gen:alph` at 13.33 px: 51.64 measured against 51.62 small-cap, 45.21 lowercase, 67.01 capitals; `epi:fact` 43.68 against 43.66, 38.17, 56.26. Upright `snk:anch-t`-style handle (`gen:alph` at 16 px): 63.43 measured against 63.46 small-cap, 61.14 lowercase.
- Since `font-synthesis: none` is set, the browser could not fabricate the capitals or the slant; the widths therefore come from the font's own `smcp` glyphs, and the italic is the embedded true italic (the first pass had no italic face, so the italic was synthesized then). Visually, the mobile screenshots show small capitals (upright ones in "snk:ker probe destination." clearly lower than full capitals, same stroke as the text).
- Limit: at 9.87 px inside the formula the italic small capitals are very small (small-capital height about 6 px); legible on the screenshot but marginal. Stroke-weight comparison against full capitals by zoomed screenshot was not possible (the zoom tool does not magnify); the identification rests on widths, tables and the disabled synthesis.

## Findings

- (1) True small capitals, italic in formulas and upright in handles and the equation number; linked handles inside formulas are clickable (H1 and H2 verified by click). The equation number "(22.1)" and "(A.21)" are small-caps style but contain only digits, so their appearance matches plain digits.
- (2) H1 equation number is beside the display and vertically centred at desktop. At the mobile preset it is not clean: the two grid rows overflow, the page scrolls horizontally (478 against 375 px) and the number overprints the formula. The `.scrollable` container scrolls inside itself (462 content in 343) and does not widen the page. The fix for the grid rows would be `overflow-x: auto` on `.equation-body` (a suggestion; not tested).
- (3) H3 accent proposal sits properly; reserved inline height is even (48 px pitch) but twice the normal pitch; the unreserved inline fraction grows its line to 44.4 px.
- (4) G1 does not test an inline line; H4's fraction fits within the line, the underbrace group causes the growth; H3's inline fraction does grow an unreserved line.
- (5) H5 renders the alphabets only with the Unicode spellings; the library `mathvariant` forms still show plain letters.
- Pagella Math loads on all six pages and is the computed family on every math element (except the deliberate G1 control).
- DPR varied between 1 and 1.667 on desktop and was 2 on the mobile preset.
- Not observed: Gecko, WebKit, zoom levels, screen-reader speech.
